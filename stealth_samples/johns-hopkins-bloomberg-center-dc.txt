                Johns Hopkins Alternative Real Estate Conference: Healthcare Real Estate            
Johns Hopkins Carey Business School’s Alternative Real Estate Asset Series will explore the growing demand, trends, and opportunities in outpatient healthcare real estate. Demand is still outpacing supply in this sector due to long-term trends, including the aging U.S. population, a broad societal focus on wellness and preventive medicine, and an ongoing shift from inpatient care to outpatient care. Free for Johns Hopkins faculty, students, and alumni.
