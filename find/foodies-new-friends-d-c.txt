Overview
Each month, a table of 10 or less connect over dinner. You sign up, and we book the restaurant! Support local businesses.
Hey locals 🎉
Our series groups together complete strangers for a fun night out. You sign up, and we book the restaurant based on your tastes. Make new friends in your city.
Each month, it's 10 people max per table! 🍱
Our mission is to host dinners in multiple cities to bring people together while supporting locally owned businesses. Check out our feature in 
Eventbrite's 2025 TRNDS report
 about embracing the micro-event trend.
This is not directly associated with the restaurant we've selected for the week, but simply based on a table reservation via Opentable. 🍴
The pin 📍 on the map is not the location but a placeholder since each location changes week to week, so please do not go to the pin.
Our pages are set to $USD.

How does it work?
*
Your registration does not include your individual food and drink purchases
. A paid ticket simply makes you more committed to actually showing up, supports our team's endeavors, and contributes to
The Comeunity Project
 (see below for info).
*These outings are intended to help people make friends. These are not dating gatherings. 🧍‍♂️ 🧍‍♀️
*If you sign up the day of, there's a risk of already reaching our maximum attendees for the night, or not having enough interest to move forward with a table reservation. Simply email ermantourageimpact@gmail.com for info prior to signing up if you'd like.
Our vision is simple: we take the no gimmicks approach to networking. No need for yet another app to take up space on your phone - just four walls and a roof and good food for people to connect :) We coordinate with you directly, human-to-human. 🤖
If you have any more questions, please contact
