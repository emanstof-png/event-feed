Future EventS
This page features local events that are:
single or non-routine live events that are scheduled more than a month ahead of when they are first announced to us,
planned events with no settled date, 
or
visiting instructors who are in town for a brief defined period and available for privates.
Listings placed on this page will remain here until the event has taken place.  Events listed here are also placed on the Capital Tangueros calendar.
