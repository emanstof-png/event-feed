			Who We Are	
Founded to foster curiosity and courage, The Explorer’s Club brings together people who believe in discovery as a way of life. Our members are hikers, scientists, historians, photographers, and lifelong learners.

The Explorers Club is a multidisciplinary society dedicated to the advancement of field research, scientific exploration and resource conservation.
