SAX Dinner Theater & Lounge
 is a vibrant destination in 
Washington
 where fine dining meets electrifying performance art. The venue hosts long-running 
burlesque shows
, gravity-defying 
aerial dance
, and themed 
bottomless brunch
 events. After dark, it transforms into an immersive nightclub experience with 
bottle service
