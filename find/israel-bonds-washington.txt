A Direct and Trusted Way to Invest in Support of Israel
For more than seventy years, individuals and institutions have invested in Israel Bonds to support Israel’s economic development while diversifying their portfolios. These general obligation bonds are used by the State of Israel when and where needed most and hold a perfect record of on-time payments of both principal and interest. 
As Israel Bonds marks 75 years of investment in Israel in 2026, we remain a trusted economic partner to the State of Israel, offering investors a direct and impactful way to invest in support of Israel while aligning with long-term financial goals.
Development Corporation for Israel (DCI), also known as Israel Bonds, is a U.S. broker dealer and member of FINRA. DCI is the exclusive underwriter of debt securities issued by the State of Israel in the United States.

Good to Know: Israel Bonds currently offer two bond options
Fixed rate bonds
, such as Jubilee and Maccabee Bonds, pay interest every May 1 and November 1, and at maturity, with the principal repaid at maturity.
Savings bonds
, including eMazel Tov, Mazel Tov, Shalom, eShalom, and Sabra, accrue interest throughout the term and pay both the principal and interest in full at maturity.
