Welcome to Jewish National Fund-USA! This region is proud to serve Maryland,
    Delaware, Northern Virginia and Greater Washington, D.C.  We have offices
    in Rockville, MD and Pikesville, MD, with three regional boards:
Greater Washington, DC.
;
Maryland
; and
Delaware
.
All across the country people are getting involved to help build a stronger,
    more secure, and more prosperous Israel for the Jewish people everywhere. You
    can be a part!
Inspired by the legacy of Theodor Herzl and guided by an ambitious strategic
    roadmap for the next decade, we see a bright, beautiful future for the land and
    people of Israel. Join us.
