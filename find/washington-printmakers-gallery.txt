Refund Policy Disclaimer
1. Prior to the Start of a Class or Workshop
Refunds will be issued if a 
minimum of seven (7) days' notice
 is provided prior to the scheduled event or program. However, refund requests made with less than seven days' notice will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis, and approval is not guaranteed. Decisions will be based on individual circumstances and the feasibility of filling the reserved spot.
2. Once a Class or Workshop Has Begun
No refunds will be issued once a class or workshop has begun, regardless of the number of sessions attended. This includes partial attendance, missed sessions, voluntary withdrawal, or personal dissatisfaction.
At the sole discretion of the Washington Print Foundation, a credit toward another class or workshop may be offered as an exceptional courtesy. Such credits are non-refundable, non-transferable, and subject to availability.
3. Cancellation by the Washington Print Foundation
If a class or workshop is canceled by the Washington Print Foundation, participants will receive a full refund.
4. Acceptance of Terms
By registering for a class or workshop, participants acknowledge and agree to the terms of this cancellation and refund policy.
