Welcome to The University Club of Washington, DC
A Tradition of Belonging
Our historic Club is one of America’s oldest and finest private city clubs. Currently ranked number 12 on the Platinum Clubs of America list and recognized among the top Distinguished City Clubs in the nation, the University Club provides our members and their families with congenial social gatherings, a rich variety of cultural, international, and educational programs, fine dining, superb athletic facilities, event space, overnight accommodations, and the opportunity to give back through our charitable foundation.
