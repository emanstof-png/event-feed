For over 100 years, the Northern Virginia Chamber (NVC) — the largest and most influential chamber in Greater Washington — has driven innovation and economic growth. NVC has championed key developments such as Metro, Dulles Airport, and George Mason University. Representing nearly 400 members, NVC continues to lead on affordability, workforce, and regional development through NOVA Roadmap and exclusive programming and events.

NVC brings the business community together to
solve regional issues.
We’re not the region’s largest business association solely due to membership numbers. We’re also the biggest because our reach extends throughout Greater Washington’s business community. This reach drives our work: we focus entirely on our region's business community and the issues it faces. It is through this work with business leaders and government officials that we leverage the power in our business community’s collective voice.

We help our region attract and retain a skilled workforce
Workforce. It’s what drives our business community and can be a make or break factor in supply chains and corporate growth. It's so vital to business that it is used as a barometer to determine a community’s health and potential. We are deeply committed to addressing our Region’s workforce challenges in creative and highly impactful ways.

I am honored to be a member of The Northern Virginia Chamber of Commerce (NOVA). At its core, NOVA is a well-connected and influential organization where our region's diverse leadership come together to influence policy and generate equitable growth in the Greater Washington DC Metropolitan Region. NOVA has positively impacted our community for 97 years, and is one of the oldest, largest, and most respected chambers of commerce in the country. NOVA provides unprecedented access to key decision-makers, the visibility to build interest and grow your company, and the ability to generate new ideas and measurable improvements in our community. NOVA remains committed, ensuring business has a voice in the legislative process, and providing tools to help businesses develop, and leverage relationships across all public and commercial sectors.
