About This Event
It’s a season of appreciation, so why don’t we take a moment to give Thanksgiving’s most featured attendent a night off: the turkey! This Shabbat, we’re breaking norms and serving up a homemade spread with everything BUT the turkey, thereby adding MoHo Arlington to the presidental pardoning turkey train since 1963. No need to bring anything. Vegetarian, gluten free, and lactose free options available! Sign up on Onetable.
