Upcoming retreats and events

Fall 2026 weekend retreat, October 29-November 1
The retreat will be held at the 
Dayspring Silent Retreat Center
 in Germantown, Maryland, just north of Washington, DC, from Thursday evening to Sunday afternoon. Retreats are silent and include many periods of seated meditation (zazen), short breaks, meals and rest periods. All meals are included in the event cost. 
The facilities include a building with a large room set up as the zendo and separate buildings with living quarters. All rooms are singles with in-room sinks. There are separate wings for men and women. Vegetarian meals will be provided by Dayspring staff; retreat participants will help with dining room setup and meal cleanup.
Space is limited to 14 participants.
More information will be available as the retreat date approaches.
