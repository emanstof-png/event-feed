Time Out says
Twins Kelly and Maize Tesfaye are the dynamic duo behind this mainstay DC jazz club. The club draws in legends like Stevie Wonder as well as local aspiring musicians. Expect live jazz music every single night as you grab a drink from the ceramic cave-inspired bar and devour some Ethiopian, Caribbean and American fare. With seating for only 65 patrons, the experience is bound to be a unique one.
