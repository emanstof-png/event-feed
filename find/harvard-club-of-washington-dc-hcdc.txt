International Networking Soiree with Champagne, Art, and Live Jazz
Thursday, May 16 from 7 to 9 pm at the Embassy Row Hotel in Washington, DC
Join members of the Ivy Social Club, the International Club of DC, the Rotary Club, Princeton Club, and other alumni clubs at the Embassy Row Hotel, one of Washington’s premiere boutique hotels in the heart of DuPont Circle, for a delightful evening of networking and socializing as you enjoy live jazz music and artwork presented by prominent local artists along with champagne specials.
This is a wonderful opportunity to network and socialize with other professionals as you make new friends. You are welcome to attend on your own or with friends! As you meet other guests enjoy champagne, wine, or specialty cocktails available for purchase at the bar.
Business or cocktail attire requested. Tickets in advance are $10 and $15 at the entrance, pending space availability.
