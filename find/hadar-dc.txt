About HADAR
The Hadar Institute is a center of Jewish life, learning, and practice that builds vibrant egalitarian communities in North America and Israel. Our vision for Jewish life is rooted in rigorous and nuanced Torah study, gender equality, meaningful Jewish practice, and the values of kindness and compassion.

Hadar's Rising Song Intensive
DECEMBER 20-23 | YESHIVAT HADAR, NYC
Guided by a faculty team of composers and scholars, Hadar’s Rising Song Intensive offers four days of musical Torah, immersive song, and skill-building. Our theme this year - Sfatai Tiftah: Sourcing our Songs’ Strength - dives into the relationship between the personal, the communal, and the Divine in accessing the wellspring of song. Register by Oct 25 at our early-bird pricing!
