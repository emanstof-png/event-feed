Zaq Landsberg: Reclining Liberty
Ongoing
Front Lawn
 MoCA Arlington and Arlington Public Art are thrilled to co-sponsor the installation of Zaq Landsberg’s celebrated Reclining Liberty on the Museum’s front lawn.
Assembly 2026: New South, Ground Truth
October 3, 2026 - January 24, 2027
New South, Ground Truth
 brings together four Atlanta-based artists in an exhibition that reframes land not as backdrop, but as evidence. Working across photography, abstraction, painting, and installation, these artists examine the Piedmont South as a site shaped by memory, power, and unresolved history.
Assembly 2026: 
Dusk in the Dawnland
October 3, 2026 - January 24, 2027
Dusk in the Dawnland
 brings together five diverse contemporary artists with deep connections to Maine,  specifically to the state’s coast and the scenic Penobscot Bay. These artists work in sculpture, textiles, video, and photography, utilizing materials that range from found objects to cutting edge technology.
Assembly 2026: Common Wealth
October 3, 2026 - January 24, 2027
Common Wealth
 uses archeology as a metaphor to consider history’s residue, how it surfaces in our present, and how artists excavate meaning from these materials. It brings together work by Beatrice Opokua Atencah, Michael Dela Dika, and Nicholas Malik Izon, three artists who were born elsewhere but now call Pennsylvania home.
Assembly 2026: to eat out their substance
October 3, 2026 - January 24, 2027
to eat out their substance
 brings together artists who intersect with municipal, civic, and technological infrastructures as the basis of their practice. Working across performance, photography, sculpture, video, sound, and conceptual intervention, these artists are authorized, unauthorized, surveilled, or otherwise entangled in the strange theater of civic life.

Shaylin Wallace: Under The Doodlescope
October 3, 2026 - November 15, 2026
Under the Doodlescope presents artist Shaylin Wallace’s abstract, intuitive doodle artworks across digital illustrations, ceramics, textiles, and painting.
