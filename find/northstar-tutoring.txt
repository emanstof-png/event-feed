Reading All Stars
Northstar’s Reading All Stars program is designed specifically to help K-8th grade students improve their reading skills while also fostering a love of reading. 
Sessions are held at the Mt. Pleasant Library from 10:30-12:00 pm on Saturdays and students work with their own tutor for one-on-one support in reading. By providing regular, individualized attention, we aim to boost both academic performance, student confidence, and a love of reading.
