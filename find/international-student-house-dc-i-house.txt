A MISSION FOR THE WORLD  
The mission of the International Student House of Washington, DC, a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization in the heart of 
Washington, DC
, is to foster intercultural dialogue, encourage lifelong connections, and promote global citizenship through a community-centered experience.
Located in the heart of Dupont Circle, I-House DC has been Home to around 
300
 residents from 
70
 countries each year for nearly a century. Over 
15,000
 individuals representing more than 
140
 countries, including the United States, have lived here since our founding in 1936.
