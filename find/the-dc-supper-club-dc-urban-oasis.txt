Washington, DC
DC Urban Oasis presents The DC Supper Club — Book Club Edition: The Happiness Reboot, an intimate supper club for anyone ready to trade inherited scripts for their own answers. Author Craig Robinson has spent years coaching executives and studying why so many people who've checked every box still feel unfulfilled. He joins us to unpack his book, The Happiness Reboot: The Path to Reclaiming Your Joy, a hands-on guide to approaching career, faith and relationships with curiosity instead of certainty. 
Over a multi-course Cuban meal crafted by Chef Hector Moya, guests will take part in an interactive conversation built to spark reflection, and maybe a little reinvention.
WHAT TO EXPECT
- Highly curated communal experience
- Multi-course dinner with the author
- Fireside chat and book signing (bring your own copy)
- Welcome beverage upon arrival
- Non-alcoholic beverages throughout
- Meet-and-greet with photo opportunity
- (Optional) Curated Cocktail & Wine Drink Package
