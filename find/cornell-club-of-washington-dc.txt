Blacksmithing Workshop Thanksgiving Weekend
When
11/28/2026
	9:00 AM - 12:00 PM
Location
22611 Markey Ct, Ste 106, Sterling, VA 20166
Spaces left
3
Event registration is now closed. We have reached capacity. 
Volunteer to co-host (it's quite easy) and we will be able to run the event in the future.
-----
Join Cornell Alumni on Thanksgiving Saturday early morning for a 3-hour blacksmithing workshop at Lawless Forge in Sterling, VA.
Expect this event to fill up fast!
Date: 
Saturday, Nov 28, 2026    
(Thanksgiving Weekend.)
Time:
 9:00 am (sharp!)  
$95 fee
 ($105 non -members) 
covers all materials
 and you'll take home the BBQ fork you make out of raw steel. 
Registration fee is 
non-refundable.
Please arrive 15 minutes prior to the start of class
:
 "To provide safety training that protects all of Cornell Club's participants (and some are as young as 14) , we will not allow late entrance. Be on time; it's imperative!  Come early! Bring coffee and socialize!
PARKING -
 Please park on the left side of the building, the larger parking lot. Carpooling is highly encouraged.
Every participant is required to fill out this
digital waiver
 - 
https://lawless-forge-sterling.checkfront.com/reserve/document/?template_id=1&id=ZXDV-260825
What to Expect:
Starting with raw steel, each person will use fire, hammer, and anvil to forge a wrought iron custom piece to take home
"It's gonna be empowering. It's gonna be dirty. It's gonna be totally new. Don’t worry, it's nothing you can't handle." 
No experience required, just a bit of strength for pounding and twisting the steel; hand-eye coordination always helps.
Set inside a large workshop, we are indoors.
There are open flame forges at 1000 degrees hot, and participants carrying hot iron with tongs. Pay close attention to safety instructions and how to properly maintain distance from participants as you carry the implements. 
This event is not for children younger than 14. But it's a fantastic 
memory-maker
 for families with older kids, parents with students home from college for the long weekend, and Cornellians who want to bring guests.
What to Wear and bring:
Closed-toe shoes/boots (sneakers are prefect): ABSOLUTELY NO SANDALS/HEELS
Jeans or shorts
Any cotton tee-shirt or long-sleeve shirt, (bonus if it says "Cornell" on it)
Sweatshirt (optional)
Earplugs if the banging will bother you
Avoid synthetics when possible
Safety gear including glasses, gloves, and aprons will be provided.
Bring a water bottle.
Take photos.
Feel free to email, call or 
text
 Lawless Forge with any questions about      the workshop.
Lawless Forge at 
22611 Markey Ct, Suite 106, Sterling, VA 20166       
sterling@lawlessforge.com
www.lawlessforge.com
Hosted by Maggie, Cornell '86  at     
communityservice@cornellclubdc.org
Visit this LOGISTICS page for helpful updates that ensure successful registration and preparation:  
https://sites.google.com/view/ccw-event-details/logistics
