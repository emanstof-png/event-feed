The International Trade Association
WITA is a non-profit, non-partisan organization and the world’s premier trade forum, serving as a central hub connecting trade professionals across the United States and around the world through open, robust discussion of international trade policy.

Washington International Trade Foundation
The Washington International Trade Foundation (WITF) is a 501(c)3 charitable organization supporting WITA’s educational mission, with its own Board of Directors and bylaws, managed by WITA staff.
