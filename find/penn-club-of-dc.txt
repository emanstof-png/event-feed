Digital Revolution in Healthcare Panel
Hosted 6/11/2020
Hear from a panel of experts as they address the question "How Will the Digital Revolution in Healthcare Factor into Our Return to a "New Normal"? Our experts dress the positives, such as greater access to affordable healthcare, a more comprehensive picture of the patient with linked medical records, predictive artificial intelligence for better patient outcomes... and the negatives, such as privacy and security exposure, the digital learning curve and possible “depersonalization” of medicine. 
Panelists include:
 Moderator:
•	Lisa Katzman, C’87, 
Director of Marketing, Optisave LLC, Healthcare IT Advisory firm specializing in cost reduction and performance improvement
 Speakers:
•	Chris Brandt, WG'08,
 Founder & CEO, Audacious Inquiry (Ai), an information technology and policy company focused on connected healthcare
•	Sunil Budhrani, C'95,
 Chief Executive Officer at Innovation Health - Aetna, a CVS Health Company & INOVA Health System partnership
•	Genevieve Morris, 
Owner, Integral Health Strategies (IHS), Health IT and strategic consulting services
