Third Thursdays
On every third Thursday of the month, Hillyer is open for extended hours between 5 and 8 p.m. During this time, visitors can view our current exhibitions and/or participate in public programs and events. You can also enjoy Third Thursday at The Studio Gallery, The Phillips Collection, Washington Studio School, ADA Art Gallery, Heurich House, Vika Art Gallery, and The Korean Cultural Center.

The Art of Collecting
September 17, 2026
5:00 pm
 – 8:00 pm
Third Thursday
During our monthly “Third Thursday” programming, visitors will explore their taste in art, learn from experts about the art of collecting, and make their final judgment when deciding on a work of art they can add to their collection.
5 to 6 p.m.
A Matter of Taste
Aspiring collectors are invited to join us for champagne, while spending some meaningful time in the galleries to browse the works of art on view throughout the auction. Loosely inspired by Immanuel Kant’s notion of taste, collectors are encouraged to find one work of art that speaks to them on a subjective level, yet rising to the level of the “beautiful,” thus achieving the highest form of aesthetic judgement.
6:15 to 7:15 p.m.
The Art of Collecting Panel Discussion
After cultivating one’s taste for the beautiful in art, Juanita Hardy, co-founder of the Millennium Arts Salon, will facilitate a discussion about the art of collecting. The panelists will share their thoughts on how art they admired in a gallery or public art space becomes a part of their personal collection. Panelists: Steven Clay, George Hemphill, Joan Weber, and Nancy Hirshbein
7:15 to 8 p.m.
From Judgment to Collecting
Following the panel discussion, collectors and art enthusiasts will be encouraged to place bids on a work of art that warrants their highest and deepest admiration. Anyone who places a bid will receive a free Cosmopolitan membership to International Arts and Artists, plus swag such tote bags, buttons, and postcards.

A Digital Sound and Sonification Workshop with Tara Youngborg
June 18, 2026
6:15 pm
 – 8:00 pm
Our featured solo artist, Tara Youngborg, will lead a digital sound workshop with a beginner-friendly coding language. In this workshop, you will be able to create a web page that can turn information from your computer or phone input into sounds, turning your device and the information it collects from your inputs into an instrument! Participants will need to bring a laptop, phone, or other device that can access the internet. Hillyer will provide Wifi access, if needed. Headphones are encouraged. 
Free for Members ($10 for nonmembers).
