Are You Now or Have You Ever Been?
Arena Stage | Washington, DC
Straight from Off-Broadway comes a timely and important play — Eric Bentley's ARE YOU NOW OR HAVE YOU EVER BEEN, directed by Tony Award winner Anna D. Shapiro. This electrifying docudrama drops you into the tense world of the 1940s House Committee on Un-American Activities hearings, where fame offered no protection and political pressure could end a career. Using only the original transcripts, the play features the words of artists like Arthur Miller, Jerome Robbins, Paul Robeson, Elia Kazan, Lillian Hellman, Abe Burrows, and Lionel Stander as they faced impossible choices: defend their integrity, protect their careers, or betray their peers. And yet the probing, chilling questions posed by the committee are as powerful as the testimonies themselves, revealing a climate of intimidation and moral conflict that resonates far beyond its time.

Peni Candra Rini with Shahzad Ismaily & Andy McGraw / Alex Koi x Kirin McElwain
Culture | Washington, DC
Saturday October 24 doors at 6:30, show at 7 $20-30 TICKETS Peni Candra Rini (b. 1983) is a renowned Javanese singer, educator, and composer fusing Javanese sufi poetry with contemporary composition for Indonesian and Western instruments. She is a master of several traditional forms (primarily gamelan) and is regarded as one of Indonesia's most daring young composers, one of only a handful of female composers in the country. She has performed extensively internationally, including several world tours as the featured singer for Robert Wilson's I La Galigo. She is a recipient of grants from the Asian Cultural Council, is a two-time grantee of the US State Department's One Beat and Fulbright programs, and in 2022 was named an Aga Khan Foundation laureate of the arts. In 2023 she became the first woman to be commissioned to compose a work for the Mangkunegaran court of Central Java and in 2024 was bestowed the court name Raden Nganten Tumenggung, the highest honor offered by the Raja. In October 2026 Kronos will release an album of her string quartets on the Phenotypic label, some of which they have performed on NPR's Tiny Desk series. Shahzad Ismaily is a Brooklyn-based musician, composer, engineer and founder of Figure 8 Recording. Born to Pakistani immigrant parents, he grew up in a wholly bicultural household. Exploring improvisation, tonal shifts and rhythmic movement, the Grammy nominated artist (Love In Exile, Best Alternative Jazz Album) is often referred to as the "Musician's favorite Musician". Over the last thirty years he has played electric bass, drums, percussion, guitar, synthesizers and all manner of sound-makers procured in life's travels. Ismaily has done work for dance and theater pieces, such as the film Frozen River (Oscar-nominated and Sundance award-winning) and Inkboat (a butoh crew from California/Switzerland). Andy McGraw is Professor and Chair of Music at the University of Richmond in Virginia. He is the author of Radical Traditions: Reimagining Culture in Balinese Experimental Music (Oxford 2013) and Music as Ethics (Oxford 2023). He has co-edited two volumes on Indonesian music: Performing Indonesia, with Sumarsam (Smithsonian 2014) and Sounding Out the State of Indonesian Music, with Chris Miller (Cornell 2022). He has published numerous articles on music and ethics as well as analytical pieces on rhythm in Balinese, Javanese, and Cuban musics. In Richmond he facilitates community gamelan and stringband ensembles and runs a music program in the Virginia prison system.

Count Gore De Vol presents THE EVIL DEAD - 45th Anniversary
AFI Silver Theatre and Cultural Center | Silver Spring, MD
Count Gore De Vol presents THE EVIL DEAD (1981) – 45th Anniversary Join us for some creepy good fun when local TV legend Count Gore De Vol (Dick Dyszel) returns to present another cult favorite in the spirit of theoriginal CREATURE FEATURE broadcasts, including vintage clips from the Count’s vault and interactive fun and games. Sam Raimi’s original vision for this slasher horror film was an indie experiment that would inspire a genre; he used the low budget to his advantage and injected humor into the Satan-laden narrative. Five college students, including Ash Williams (Bruce Campbell), travel to a cabin in the Tennessee woods where they encounter evil spirits emanating from an audio tape and embodying the environment around them, resulting in increasingly gruesome and gory deaths. The film was a mild box-office success, but was lauded by critics, and even screened at the prestigious Cannes Film Festival in 1982. It would go on to spawn a franchise, including sequels, video games, a TV show and a big-budget remake. RATED NC-17 This restoration was scanned in 4K from the original camera negative.
