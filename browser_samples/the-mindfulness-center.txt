FAQ
We strive to provide the best possible experience for our clients by staying up-to-date with technology advances so that you can stay active and connected through online classes and workshops.
What platform do I use to take your online classes?
We use  MindBody Online for you t0 register to classes. 
Classes are conducted through Zoom.
How do I register for a class?
Sign in HERE to access your personalized account. We recommend doing this now and not waiting until you are ready to join a class, as it may take a few minutes to set up your account.
Set your email account so that messages from “hello@fitgrid.com” always arrive in your inbox, and are never marked as spam. Here’s a guide for how to do this in Gmail.
View our class schedule online, register for the class you want, and choose your membership and payment options. For right now, classes are FREE/By-Donation.
Premium Vitality Breath classes are $25 registration
You will receive a confirmation email from FitGrid soon after.
60 minutes prior to the scheduled class start time, you will receive another email from FitGrid with a unique link to access the live stream. Not seeing the email? Search your spam and promotions folders. Look for subject lines containing the phrase “live-stream link”.
Click your unique link a few minutes before class is scheduled to start.
If you have the Zoom application on your device already, you’ll join immediately, otherwise you will be prompted to download Zoom.
Register early for class. You can only register for classes PRIOR to their start time. Once a class has started, you will not be allowed to register for the class.
I already have a membership or pass, how do I use it with the new system?
We are working to integrate passes with our new system. In the meantime, when you register for a class, it may ask you to pay, but when you check out, there should be the option to select “A Free Community Class Pass” option at $0 (or to make a donation). Please choose that option, and you will be registered for class properly.
