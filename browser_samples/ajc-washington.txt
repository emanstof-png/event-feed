FAQs

Is this donation tax deductible?

Yes! American Jewish Committee (AJC) is a not-for-profit organization recognized as tax-exempt under Internal Revenue Code section 501(c)(3). Donations to AJC are tax exempt to the extent permitted by law. Qualified donations will be receipted in the following calendar year.

Can I donate by mail?

You can mail your check, payable to AJC, to: 

AMERICAN JEWISH COMMITTEE 

Mail Code: 6760 

P.O. Box 7247 

Philadelphia, PA 19170-0001

Who can I contact for assistance in making a donation?

The AJC team can help you make a donation safely and securely. We are available Mon-Thu, 9:00 AM-5:30 PM ET, and Fri, 9:00 AM-3:00 PM ET. You can call 212.891.1454 or email contribute@ajc.org. Thank you for your support of AJC.

Can I make a donation using stock?

Absolutely! For stock transfer instructions, please call 212.891.1454 or email contribute@ajc.org.

How can I learn more about the different ways to support AJC’s work?

Visit AJC.org/supportourwork to learn more about the opportunities available to you as a member of an AJC Donor Recognition Society. Visit AJC.org/plannedgiving to learn more about making a planned gift to AJC.
