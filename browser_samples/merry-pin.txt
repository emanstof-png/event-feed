FAQs

We offer three formats of activities at Merry Pin:

Make & Take

Self-guided, "craft cafe-style" activities—there is no facilitator or instructor. We provide all of the supplies and written instructions for you to complete the craft at your own pace. You can drop in at any time during the Make & Take hours to participate.

Workshops

Workshops are classroom-style opportunities, led by an instructor.

Events

From trivia to movie nights, we offer a variety of events at Merry Pin. Some are free (RSVPs always appreciated) and others are ticketed.
