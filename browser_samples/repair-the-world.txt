Volunteer

We are the Jewish service movement.
 Volunteering is how we express our Judaism—meeting urgent needs, building community, and creating joyful experiences rooted in learning and action. Through Jewish service, we bring people together across backgrounds to foster connection and healing, in pursuit of a just world.

                                        What The Cluck? A Sacred Shechita Retreat                                    

                                Oct 25, 2026                            

Atlanta

                                    Food Justice                                

                            Begins: 12:00 PM (ET)                                    
 Ends: 11:00 AM (ET)                                

In Parashat Vayera, Abraham runs to his herd to prepare a meal for three strangers. The week we read it, join us on a Jewish farm in North Georgia, October 25–27, for What the Cluck!, a hands-on retreat rooted in shechita, the Jewish ritual of sacred slaughter. Guided by a certified shochet, we'll learn the practice by processing chickens together, grounded in tza'ar ba'alei chayim (compassion for living creatures) and shomrei adamah (guardians of the earth). We'll also tour the farm, explore regenerative farming, herbalism, and homesteading, and gather for a BBQ and bonfire. We camp on site, and everyone helps cook one shared meal. Geared to folks in their 20s and 30s, and open to all, Jewish or not. Sliding scale $25–$100 covers meals.

In partnership with MEOR, we'll gather for brunch, Jewish learning, and a chance to support Family Table's essential work. Together, we'll pack and deliver kosher food boxes to seniors, including Holocaust survivors, in Allston, Brighton, and Brookline. Our tradition calls us to act on both food insecurity and senior isolation, and for many of the people we visit, a delivery means a full fridge and a friendly face at the door.

Logistics: In addition to signing up with MEOR you must register with JF&CS and fill out the CORI form. You'll find further instructions on MEOR's sign-up page. If you have a car, we need drivers!

                                        The Breakfast Club                                    

                                Oct 27, 2026                            

Detroit

                                    Food Justice                                

                            Begins: 6:00 PM (ET)                                    
 Ends: 8:00 PM (ET)                                

Join Repair the World Detroit for The Breakfast Club as we launch our monthly service program addressing food access and housing security. Each month, we gather to assemble cereal packs for The Pope Francis Center to share with unhoused community members in Detroit. A good breakfast is a simple thing, and for too many people in our city, it isn’t guaranteed. Jewish tradition calls this work tzedek, justice: making sure every person has what they need to live with dignity. When we show up together month after month, we build relationships with our partners, with each other, and with the city we call home. Come once or make it part of your monthly rhythm. Together, we take action in pursuit of a just world, one breakfast at a time.

FAQs

What impact does my donation make?

Your gift powers meaningful Jewish service learning and vibrant Jewish communities across the country. See what donors like you make possible: https://report.werepair.org/

Is this donation tax deductible?

Yes. Repair the World is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization. Your donation is tax-deductible within U.S. law. You’ll receive an email receipt to keep for your records.

What is Repair the World's Tax ID (EIN)?

Repair the World's Employer Identification Number (EIN) is 36-4524686.

How can I donate?

You can give online, by check (payable to Repair the World, 25 Broadway, 17th Fl, NY, NY 10004), or via stock (email us to learn more), real estate (through Investing in Communities), or platforms like Goodnation, My Tzedakah, Facebook, and Instagram. Many donors create Facebook fundraisers: https://rpr.world/fundraise. We're exploring crypto gifts—email development@weRepair.org to learn more.

Can my employer match my donation?

Possibly! Check your company’s matching gift policy and double your impact: werepair.org/employer-donation-matching

Who can I contact with questions or special requests?

Email development@weRepair.org for donation support, to request a physical acknowledgment, or to learn more about how your giving fuels thriving Jewish life.
