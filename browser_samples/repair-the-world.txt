Volunteer

We are the Jewish service movement. Volunteering is how we express our Judaism—meeting urgent needs, building community, and creating joyful experiences rooted in learning and action. Through Jewish service, we bring people together across backgrounds to foster connection and healing, in pursuit of a just world. Join us.
