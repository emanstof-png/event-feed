Volunteer
We are the Jewish service movement.
 Volunteering is how we express our Judaism—meeting urgent needs, building community, and creating joyful experiences rooted in learning and action. Through Jewish service, we bring people together across backgrounds to foster connection and healing, in pursuit of a just world.

When the rain stops in Parshat Noach, Noah sends out a raven and then a dove to search for dry land. Join Shirat Hayam's Shir Hadash young adult community along the Ipswich River, where we'll support Mass Audubon's conservation efforts to protect the habitats birds and other wildlife depend on.                                         Ambassador Led: JF&CS Young Professionals Day                                    

                                Oct 18, 2026                            
Atlanta

                                    Economic Justice                                

                            Begins: 11:00 AM (ET)                                    
 Ends: 2:00 PM (ET)                                
Join us and JF&CS Atlanta at the Kosher Food Pantry to sort, check, and shelve donated food alongside other Jewish young professionals in their 20s and 30s. The pantry provides kosher and non-kosher food to JF&CS clients facing food insecurity, so the shelves we stock go straight to Atlanta families who need them. Our tradition teaches kavod habriyot, the dignity owed to every person, and that value shapes this work: a well-organized pantry means clients get nourishing food with care and respect. It's a hands-on way to meet new people and see the difference a few hours can make. No experience needed. Bring a friend and come ready to work together.
