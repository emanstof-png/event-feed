Volunteer

We are the Jewish service movement.
 Volunteering is how we express our Judaism—meeting urgent needs, building community, and creating joyful experiences rooted in learning and action. Through Jewish service, we bring people together across backgrounds to foster connection and healing, in pursuit of a just world.

Join Repair the World Detroit at the Adamah Detroit Community Summit, the annual celebration of Jewish environmental education, leadership, and climate action. Join us for a morning of service with Gift of Gratitude and Chabad of Calabasas. Together, we'll assemble hygiene bundles for young people in our community who are going through a time of transition. Volunteers will set up the space, pack and double-check bags, and write letters of encouragement alongside our teen volunteers. Want to bring supplies? Jewish tradition teaches that how we give matters as much as what we give, so every bundle goes out with a note from someone who cares.

                                                    Community Gardening with Neighbors for a Greener Harlem                                                

                                Nov 15, 2026                            

New York

                                    Environmental Justice                                

                            Begins: 2:00 PM (ET)                                    
 Ends: 4:00 PM (ET)                                

13 spots left

Community volunteer days are a JCC Harlem project, powered by Repair the World. Each month, volunteers team up with local community-based organizations to volunteer and learn about the challenges facing the neighborhood and the world. This month, volunteers will garden alongside neighbors at the NYCHA Grant Houses Shade Garden. Together, participants will explore how access to green space connects to environmental justice and neighborhood well-being, and how neighbor-led efforts strengthen community. The experience includes Jewish learning about caring for the land and one another. Meet at 125th Street, just east of Amsterdam Avenue.

Each month, volunteers team up with local community-based organizations to volunteer and learn about the challenges facing the neighborhood and the world. This month, volunteers will garden alongside neighbors at the NYCHA Grant Houses Shade Garden. Together, participants will explore how access to green space connects to environmental justice and neighborhood well-being, and how neighbor-led efforts strengthen community. The experience includes Jewish learning about caring for the land and one another. Meet at 125th Street, just east of Amsterdam Avenue.

FAQs

What impact does my donation make?

Your gift powers meaningful Jewish service learning and vibrant Jewish communities across the country. See what donors like you make possible: https://report.werepair.org/

Is this donation tax deductible?

Yes. Repair the World is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization. Your donation is tax-deductible within U.S. law. You’ll receive an email receipt to keep for your records.

What is Repair the World's Tax ID (EIN)?

Repair the World's Employer Identification Number (EIN) is 36-4524686.

How can I donate?

You can give online, by check (payable to Repair the World, 25 Broadway, 17th Fl, NY, NY 10004), or via stock (email us to learn more), real estate (through Investing in Communities), or platforms like Goodnation, My Tzedakah, Facebook, and Instagram. Many donors create Facebook fundraisers: https://rpr.world/fundraise. We're exploring crypto gifts—email development@weRepair.org to learn more.

Can my employer match my donation?

Possibly! Check your company’s matching gift policy and double your impact: werepair.org/employer-donation-matching

Who can I contact with questions or special requests?

Email development@weRepair.org for donation support, to request a physical acknowledgment, or to learn more about how your giving fuels thriving Jewish life.
