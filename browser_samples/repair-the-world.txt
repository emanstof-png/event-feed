Volunteer

We are the Jewish service movement.
 Volunteering is how we express our Judaism—meeting urgent needs, building community, and creating joyful experiences rooted in learning and action. Through Jewish service, we bring people together across backgrounds to foster connection and healing, in pursuit of a just world.

                                        Care for Casa: Winter Refresh                                    

                                Nov 22, 2026                            

Orange County

                                    Housing Justice                                

                            Begins: 1:00 PM (PT)                                    
 Ends: 3:00 PM (PT)                                

Join Repair the World Orange County and Beth David Young Professionals at Casa Youth Shelter in Los Alamitos to bring warmth to the season. Casa provides emergency shelter, counseling, and support for youth ages 12 to 17 who are homeless, runaway, or in crisis, and the space they call home deserves to feel like one. We'll organize, refresh, and care for the shelter inside and out so every corner feels warm and welcoming. Along the way, we'll learn about hachnasat orchim, the Jewish value of welcoming others, and what it means to make a home for someone who needs one.

Each winter, Temple Beth Israel in Waltham opens a warming center where neighbors can find a warm, safe place to rest, and volunteers keep it running.                                         Ambassador Led: Community Clothing Donation                                    

                                Nov 24, 2026                            

                                    Community Building                                

                            Begins: 2:00 PM (ET)                                    
 Ends: 4:00 PM (ET)                                

In partnership with Loved Center, a local organisation serving vulnerable populations in Mbale, this Ambassador-led program will feature community clothing drive and clothing swap to support low-income families and orphaned children in Namatala.

Many families in this community struggle to afford necessities, and having enough clothing is often a luxury. Children wear clothes they have outgrown, while some families rely on the same few items for years. Through this initiative, community members will gather to donate gently used clothing to sort and distribute to individuals and families in need.

New York

                                    Food Justice

The day before Thanksgiving, Broadway Community is holding a big holiday food distribution, and we’re joining them to help.

Volunteers will help neighbors shop the pantry, walking with them through the aisles as they pick out what they need for their holiday table. You’ll also help keep storage organized and shelves restocked so the distribution keeps moving all afternoon.

FAQs

What impact does my donation make?

Your gift powers meaningful Jewish service learning and vibrant Jewish communities across the country. See what donors like you make possible: https://report.werepair.org/

Is this donation tax deductible?

Yes. Repair the World is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization. Your donation is tax-deductible within U.S. law. You’ll receive an email receipt to keep for your records.

What is Repair the World's Tax ID (EIN)?

Repair the World's Employer Identification Number (EIN) is 36-4524686.

How can I donate?

You can give online, by check (payable to Repair the World, 25 Broadway, 17th Fl, NY, NY 10004), or via stock (email us to learn more), real estate (through Investing in Communities), or platforms like Goodnation, My Tzedakah, Facebook, and Instagram. Many donors create Facebook fundraisers: https://rpr.world/fundraise. We're exploring crypto gifts—email development@weRepair.org to learn more.

Can my employer match my donation?

Possibly! Check your company’s matching gift policy and double your impact: werepair.org/employer-donation-matching

Who can I contact with questions or special requests?

Email development@weRepair.org for donation support, to request a physical acknowledgment, or to learn more about how your giving fuels thriving Jewish life.
