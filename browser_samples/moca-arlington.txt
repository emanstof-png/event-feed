Public Programs

Yoga in the Galleries: Saturday Mornings
Saturdays / 11am - 12pm
Join us in the Tiffany Gallery for an inspiring yoga flow session. Registration Required.

Teen Art Prize
Submission Deadline: Friday, January 8, 11:59 PM
MoCA Arlington is proud to announce an open call for submissions for its first annual Teen Art Prize and exhibition. Eligible to all high school seniors who live or go to school in Arlington, VA.

Gentle Yoga in the Galleries: Monday Nights
Mondays / 6:30pm - 7:30pm
Unwind and invite balance in this gentle flowing yoga class. Monday nights in the beautiful Tiffany Gallery. Registration Required.

Young Explorers @ the Innovation Studio + Store
Tuesdays / 10am-12pm / Innovation Studio + Store
For young children and their caretakers, Young Explorers is a drop-in program held every Tuesday morning at the Innovation Studio + Store

MoCA on the Move: Handcrafted Holidays
Thursdays / Nov 5 - Dec 17 / 5pm - 7pm / Innovation Studio + Store
Feeling festive? Join us for holiday crafting fun, from making giant paper snowflakes to painting candlesticks. Free!

Curator-Led Friday Tours: Assembly 2026
Select Fridays / 12pm
Learn more about the exhibitions in Assembly 2026 with MoCA Arlington Senior Curator Blair Murphy. In a series of Friday tours, she will discuss the exhibitions and the overall vision for Assembly 2026.

Sound Bath in the Tiffany Gallery
Saturdays / Twice a Month / 2-3pm
Enjoy the soothing experience of a Sound Bath in the glow of the Tiffany Gallery’s stained glass windows. Offered twice a month. Registration Required.

Wellness Wednesdays @ the Innovation Studio: Intuitive Art Making
1st Wednesdays / 6:30pm-7:30pm / Innovation Studio
Catch the vibe of wellness. Every 1st Wednesday is intuitive art-making. Explore color, process, and expression in a relaxed atmosphere.

Yogalates + Matcha
Sunday / December 6 / 3PM - 4:30PM
Join yoga and pilates instructor, Chelsea Estevez, for an afternoon yogalates session followed by Matcha tasting! Register to attend.

Sagittarius Party!
Sunday / December 13 / 4pm - 7pm
Celebrate MoCA Arlington’s birthday! Planetarium shows, tarot, aura photos, costume contest, art making + more. All star signs welcome.

Meditation in the Galleries
3rd Sundays (Starting in December) / 12:30pm - 1pm
Invite mindfulness practices into your experience with art. A free program hosted the 3rd Sunday of each month.
