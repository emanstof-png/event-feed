                Whose Primary Is It Anyway? How We Choose Presidential Nominees—and What Could Change            
The Democracy Reform Primer Series turns to the presidential nomination process for a happy-hour conversation about early-state influence, competing state interests, and proposals to change how the parties choose their nominees. Drawing on a new primer by Brown University economist Brian Knight, the discussion will examine how early results influence later voters, why states compete for a place at the front of the calendar, and what research tells us about alternatives to the current system.
