Frequently Asked Questions
What kind of events happen at Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9?
Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9 hosts a variety of events, including comic book clubs, figure drawing sessions, competitive card game tournaments, and local flea markets.
Where is Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9 located?
You can find Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9 at 2010 P Street NW, 2nd floor, in Washington, conveniently located near the Dupont Circle metro station.
Do I need to pay an entry fee for figure drawing at Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9?
Yes, figure drawing sessions at Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9 typically have a $15 entry fee, and tickets can be purchased via the link in their bio.
Are art supplies provided for workshops at Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9?
For figure drawing sessions at Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9, basic art supplies are provided, though they encourage attendees to bring their own materials.
How can I participate in Magic: The Gathering events at Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9?
Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9 hosts regular Magic: The Gathering tournaments and beginner learn-to-play sessions; you can secure your spot by purchasing tickets through the link in their bio.
What's happening at Fantom Comics this week?
Fantom Comics in Washington is hosting four events this week, starting with MTG Learn-to-Play Night on Monday at 18:30. You can also catch the Pokémon Trade & Play Night on Tuesday at 18:30, the Higher Learning Book Club on Wednesday at 18:30, and the Crabrun Indie Game Launch, which is Free, on Friday at 18:30.
What's on at Fantom Comics this weekend?
This weekend at Fantom Comics in Washington, you can attend the Crabrun Indie Game Launch on Friday, October 3 at 18:30. This event is Free to attend and features an atmospheric indie arcade challenge.
What events are coming up at Fantom Comics this month?
Fantom Comics in Washington has three distinct community events scheduled for September. Highlights include the beginner-friendly MTG Learn-to-Play Night, the casual Pokémon Trade & Play Night, and the 21+ Higher Learning Book Club hosted at YANA Wellness.
