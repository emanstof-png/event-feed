About 
the place
Situated on the second floor at 2010 P Street NW in Washington, 
Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9
 functions as a vibrant community center. Visitors find a wide selection of 
comics
, 
manga
, and 
manhwa
, alongside regular programming like 
book clubs
, 
figure drawing
 sessions, and 
digital collaging
 workshops. The space serves as an active venue for competitive 
card games
 including Magic: The Gathering, Pokémon, and Yu-Gi-Oh!, while also hosting local flea markets, author talks, and festive drag shows.

STEPHANIE WILLIAMS In Conversation
Good to know
What kind of events happen at Fantom Comics?
Mostly community events (77 of 138), talks (35 of 138) and workshops (28 of 138), counted from every event MATO has listed here.
Are events at Fantom Comics free?
11 of 53 events with known prices were marked free entry. Each event page shows the price when it is known.
What kind of events happen at Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9?
Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9 hosts a variety of events, including comic book clubs, figure drawing sessions, competitive card game tournaments, and local flea markets.
Where is Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9 located?
You can find Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9 at 2010 P Street NW, 2nd floor, in Washington, conveniently located near the Dupont Circle metro station.
Do I need to pay an entry fee for figure drawing at Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9?
Yes, figure drawing sessions at Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9 typically have a $15 entry fee, and tickets can be purchased via the link in their bio.
Are art supplies provided for workshops at Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9?
For figure drawing sessions at Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9, basic art supplies are provided, though they encourage attendees to bring their own materials.
How can I participate in Magic: The Gathering events at Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9?
Fantom Comics @ SPX M8-9 hosts regular Magic: The Gathering tournaments and beginner learn-to-play sessions; you can secure your spot by purchasing tickets through the link in their bio.
What events are coming up at Fantom Comics this month?
Fantom Comics in Washington has four events in November, mixing tabletop games, Pokémon, a craft workshop, and a book club. Standouts include Wingspan Night: European Expansion on Monday November 23 at 6:30pm ($10) and the free Pokémon Nite: Play, Trade & TCG on Tuesday November 24 from 6:30–9:30pm. Also on the calendar are the Korean Lover's Knot Workshop on Saturday November 28 at 6:30pm ($40) and the Interfaith Book Club on Sunday November 29 at 6:30pm.
What's on at Fantom Comics this weekend?
Fantom Comics in Washington has two events this weekend. The Korean Lover's Knot Workshop is on Saturday November 28 at 6:30pm, with tickets at $40. On Sunday November 29 at 6:30pm, the Interfaith Book Club meets to discuss Marian Heretic vol. 1.
What's happening at Fantom Comics this week?
Fantom Comics in Washington has four events this week. Wingspan Night: European Expansion is on Monday November 23 at 6:30pm ($10), and Pokémon Nite: Play, Trade & TCG runs Tuesday November 24 from 6:30–9:30pm and is free.
