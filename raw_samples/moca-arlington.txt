Public Programs
Yoga in the Galleries: Saturday Mornings
Saturdays / 9:30am - 10:30am & 11am - 12pm
Join us in the Tiffany Gallery for an inspiring yoga flow session. Bring a journal along with your mat!
Gentle Yoga in the Galleries: Monday Nights
Mondays / 6:30pm - 7:30pm
Unwind and invite balance in this gentle flowing yoga class. Monday nights in the beautiful Tiffany Gallery.
Young Explorers @ the Innovation Studio + Store
Tuesdays / 10am-12pm / Innovation Studio + Store
For young children and their caretakers, Young Explorers is a drop-in program held every Tuesday morning at the Innovation Studio + Store
Wellness Wednesdays @ the Innovation Studio: Intuitive Art Making
1st Wednesdays / 6:30pm-7:30pm / Innovation Studio
Catch the vibe of wellness. Every 1st Wednesday is intuitive art-making. Explore color, process, and expression in a relaxed atmosphere.
Sound Bath in the Tiffany Gallery
Saturdays / Twice a Month / 2-3pm
Join us for this lovely and relaxing event: a sound bath in the wondrous Tiffany Gallery.
MoCA on the Move: Fall Family Days!
Sundays / Sep 13 - Oct 25 / 10am - 1pm / Metropolitan Park
Fall days are made for art in the park! Enjoy live music, hands-on art, games, and more. New activities and musicians each week. Free and open to all ages!
Death and how to reheat your old nachos: a mini sculpture workshop – FREE!
THURSDAY / OCTOBER 29 / 6:00PM - 7:30PM
Create playful or thought-provoking miniature sculptures by combining small found objects with air dry clay.
Día De Los Muertos Celebration – An Evening Picnic!
Sunday / November 1 / 5PM - 8PM
An evening picnic on the lawn of MoCA Arlington with family-friendly activities, vendors, food trucks, and folklórico + mariachi performance. Free.
MoCA on the Move: Handcrafted Holidays
Thursdays / Nov 5 - Dec 17 / 5pm - 7pm / Innovation Studio + Store
Feeling festive? Join us for holiday crafting fun, from making giant paper snowflakes to painting candlesticks. Free!
Coffee + Craft
Saturday / November 7 / 9AM - 10:30AM
For Adults: Bring in an idea or in-progress project for a social crafting session at the Innovation Studio + Store! Coffee and tea provided!   Work, relax, and chat with …
Mindful Drawing + Yoga Workshop
Sunday / November 8 / 3PM - 4:30PM
Join artist, Lindsay Mueller, and yoga instructor, Chelsea Estevez, for an afternoon of creativity combining yoga and drawing! Tickets required.
Social Series: Pet Portrait Watercolors with Alexandra Schmeling
SATURDAY / NOVEMBER 14 / 6:30PM - 8:30PM
Learn how to paint your pet in watercolor in this 2-hour artist-led workshop!
Artist Talk: Emily Fussner
Saturday / November 14 / 2:00 PM
Join MoCA Arlington Resident Artist Emily Fussner for a gallery talk in “Some Shining Coil of Wind,” the artist’s current solo exhibition on view in the Wyatt Resident Gallery.
Yogalates + Matcha
Sunday / December 6 / 3PM - 4:30PM
Join yoga and pilates instructor, Chelsea Estevez, for an afternoon yogalates session followed by Matcha tasting! Register to attend.
