Zeitgeist 2025 | Literature Festival | Strangers at Home
Friday, October 24, 2025
6:30 PM
8:30 PM
                Goethe-Institut Washington
(map)
Google Calendar
ICS
In collaboration with the 
Festival Neue Literatur (FNL)
 in New York, 
Zeitgeist Literature Festival 
in Washington DC presents
 "Strangers at Home", 
which will feature readings by and a conversation among authors 
Alois Hotschnig (AT), Ariane Koch (CH), and Khuê Phạm (DE). 
What are the obligations and limits of hospitality? When and how does familiarity tip into contempt? Can we ever truly know the people closest to us? Can we truly know our own slippery selves? Why, as the narrator in 
Overstaying
 asks, do “our minds always seek out what’s known in the unknown, instead of surrendering to the unknown”?
