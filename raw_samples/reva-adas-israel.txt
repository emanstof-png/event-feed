Reva
 is Adas Israel’s community for Jewish adults in their 20s, 30s, and early 40s. It is a place to build meaningful relationships, explore Jewish life, and find a spiritual home.
Whether you’re gathering for Shabbat, learning something new, or simply looking for community, Reva meets you where you are. Rooted in the depth of Adas Israel, Reva is a community where you can cultivate Jewish values and practices, deepen your sense of belonging, and discover what it means to be part of something larger than yourself.
In a world that is constantly changing, Reva is a home, a foundation, and a launching pad. It is a place to build community, nurture your Jewish identity, and carry the relationships, traditions, and wisdom you find here into whatever comes next.
The name 
Reva
 means 
quarter
—a nod to this unique quarter of life, a season of becoming when many of us are asking who we are, where we belong, and the kind of lives and communities we hope to build.
Welcome to the next quarter of Jewish life.

Meet with Rabbi Anina Dassa
Hi! I'm Rabbi Anina Dassa.
I'm originally from Los Angeles, where I grew up at Camp Ramah in California. Before becoming a rabbi, I studied Interarts Performance at the University of Michigan, where I earned my BFA by combining art, theater, and music. I was ordained by the Jewish Theological Seminary in 2025, where I also earned a Certificate in Spiritual and Pastoral Care and Counseling. Along the way, I've been fortunate to learn and serve in a wide variety of communities, including Yeshivat Hadar, Princeton University, and as a chaplain intern with older adults in New York. Most recently, I served as a Rabbinic Fellow at B'nai Jeshurun (BJ) in New York City, where I worked closely with the young professional community and served on the leadership team for its musical Kabbalat Shabbat and High Holiday services. I loved helping create prayer experiences that were joyful, participatory, and spiritually meaningful, while also building opportunities for young professionals through learning and community. What excites me most about Reva is creating Jewish spaces that are warm, joyful, spiritually alive, and genuinely welcoming. I love bringing people together through music, thoughtful conversation, and traditions that feel both rooted and relevant. Outside of work, I’m usually singing show tunes in the shower, making art, spending time with my husband, Judah, or hanging out with our two cats. I'm so excited to get to know you and build a vibrant, joyful community together through Reva.

Hesed & Social Action
			The Hesed Committee is a collaborative effort of our clergy, staff, and volunteers to reach out to and support Adas members who are alone, hungry, convalescing, infirm, or newly arrived. Our goal is to ensure that all members of our community have an opportunity to have regular, friendly, personal contact with fellow congregants.

The Wellness Center @ Adas Israel
			The Wellness Center @ Adas Israel is thrilled to welcome you to a sacred place for communal gathering, mindfulness, and reflection. We invite community members to open themselves to wonder, awe, and Jewish ritual practice, seeking a deeper connection with one's inner self and fellow travelers. Through meditation, yoga, ritual immersion, and other embodied practices we seek to help create wholeness in the realm of the body, mind and spirit. As you explore multiple dimensions of the Jewish tradition on your journey we seek to be an avenue to help open spaces, ancient and modern, to elevate your spiritual and religious practices. Please join us to create expanse, release, connect and engage in our bodies, our lives and the world around us.
