Play Reading and Discussion : 18.11.2026, Václav Havel’s "Protest"
Wed, 18 Nov 2026 00:00:00 +0200
This series marks the 90th anniversary of Havel's birth, led by the Embassy of the Czech Republic and partners in fall 2026. Through theater, public dialogue, and youth creativity, the project honors Havel’s lifelong defense of human dignity and the power of truth. The audience will explore the moral courage of those who speak out under repression, examine today's challenges to free expression, and embrace their own capacity to spark change. Inspired by Havel's conviction that even one voice can make a difference, this program invites our community to actively reflect on what it means to safeguard freedom in our time.<br />

Film Screening : 11.10.2026, Nö
Sun, 11 Oct 2026 00:00:00 +0200
<h3 class="cke-h3">German Cinema: Nö&nbsp;</h3>
Director: Dietrich Brüggemann<br />
Germany / 2021 / 119’<br />
<br />
Dina and Michael are in their early thirties. He is a doctor, she is an actress. They are happy together until Michael thinks about breaking up. She says: NOPE (NÖ). In thirteen episodes, we then accompany our two heroes while they maneuver through life over the years and try to not lose sight of their love. "NÖ" is a film about love, how to find love and our struggles to live with love. It's a film about a generation in their thirties, who have everything, who have tried everything but who often still fail silently. The film exposes hidden states of mind and at the same time shows the important impact they have on our lives.<br />
