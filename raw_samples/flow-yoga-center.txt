Cathedral  Yoga
A truely special experience
Experience Yoga & Pilates at the Washington National Cathedral   
These thoughtfully guided classes invite you to slow down, move with intention, and reconnect in one of Washington, DC’s most breathtaking and sacred spaces.  Classes are held throughout the year beneath soaring vaulted ceilings and luminous stained glass, and  outside in the Cathedral’s peaceful, wooded amphitheater. All skill levels are welcome to join our 60-minute sessions under the guidance of experienced 
Flow Yoga Center
’s Teaching
 Faculty
Reserve early as these experiences will sell out.   (Flow Members, email 
happiness@flowyogacenter.com
 for your discount code.)

Class Themes 
Outdoor Classes
September 12 – 
Open Sky, Open Heart Yoga — Begin the fall season with an uplifting outdoor flow beneath the Cathedral. (led by Cayla)
September 19 – 
Strong from the Center Pilates— Build strength, stability, and energy with an invigorating outdoor Pilates practice. (led by Roxy)
September 26 – 
Find Your Balance Yoga— Welcome the shift into fall with a grounding flow for balance and renewal.
(led by Kate)
October 3 – 
Root & Rise Yoga— Ground down, stretch out, and move with the changing season.
(led by Katrina)
October 10 –  
Fall Strength — A crisp-air Pilates practice designed to leave you strong, energized, and refreshed. 
(led by Jessica)
October 17 – 
Breathe & Flow in the Season — Slow down, breathe deeply, and soak up autumn at the Cathedral.
(led by Debra)
October 24 – 
Core & Calm — Strengthen from within while finding a little space and stillness outdoors.
(led by - coming soon)
October 31 – 
 Halloween Glow Up Yoga — Close out the outdoor season in your favorite costume with a feel-good Halloween morning flow.
(led by Griffen)
Indoor
October 13 - Led by Debra & Cayla -  
Reap the Harvest.  Join us for an evening of yoga beneath the Cathedral’s soaring arches—a chance to slow down, stretch out, and make a little room for yourself. In this one hour practice, we will reflect on all the seeds you have planted thus far and celebrate and recognize the harvest of your perseverance, determination, and faithfulness.
October 15 - Led by Debra & Griffen  - 
Live cello & harmonium and the beauty of the Cathedral all around you. Imagine the music filling the space as you move, then settling into a quiet final rest beneath those magnificent ceilings.
