 workshops and special events
Our workshops offer deep dives for knowledge and practice that focus on a particular area of interest, led by guest experts or our own experienced teaching staff.  We also feature occasional fun yoga classes for kids and families, holiday pop-ups, and donations-only classes to support good causes. Don’t see something you’d love to learn about or experience more deeply? Give us an email and we’ll get creative with you!

                Yoga for Osteoporosis and Osteopenia with Guest Teacher Gernot Huber
November 21, 2026 – November 22, 2026
Learn Dr. Loren Fishman’s evidence-based yoga method for osteoporosis and osteopenia with certified instructor Gernot Huber. This two-session workshop focuses on improving bone mineral density, posture, balance, coordination, and safer movement patterns to help reduce the risk of falls and fractures. You’ll learn both morning and evening yoga sequences, with Session 2 building on the foundation established in Session 1. No prior yoga experience is required. Particpants must have attended Session 1 in order to participate in Session 2.

                Yoga for Functional Fitness with Catha Pavloff 
December 12, 2026
Functional fitness prepares your body for the movements and demands of everyday life, helping you move with greater ease, strength, and confidence. In this workshop, explore seven key functional movements and learn how to incorporate them creatively into your yoga practice.                 Nourish your Inner Rivers: Yoga for Lymphatic Balance with Marie Moran, RN
November 8, 2026
Discover how gentle movement, breathwork, and relaxation can support your body's natural lymphatic flow. Explore the lymphatic system's role in wellness while learning simple self-massage techniques and breathing practices that encourage circulation. Enjoy a nourishing yoga practice and restorative relaxation designed to promote vitality, ease, and balance. Leave feeling refreshed and empowered with practical self-care tools you can incorporate into your daily routine to support your overall well-being.

                Amir's Yoga Therapy for Low Back Pain 
November 14, 2026
Amir will teach you how to create a customized routine to address your specific low back dysfunctions and related pains.  He'll review the anatomy of the lower back and sacroiliac joint, as well as common misalignments, muscles spasms, compression, and disc issues. Using yoga therapeutics, as well as techniques from Egoscue, Somatics, and Thai Yoga Massage, Amir will offer tools to self-evaluate and find ease from both chronic and acute conditions.
