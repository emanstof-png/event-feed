 workshops and special events
Our workshops offer deep dives for knowledge and practice that focus on a particular area of interest, led by guest experts or our own experienced teaching staff.  We also feature occasional fun yoga classes for kids and families, holiday pop-ups, and donations-only classes to support good causes. Don’t see something you’d love to learn about or experience more deeply? Give us an email and we’ll get creative with you!

                Flow from Gratitude to Grounding with Catha Pavloff
November 29, 2026
A yoga flow class to help you transition from Thanksgiving to the December holidays.
This time of year can be overwhelming for many of us. Expectations compete with reality. Holiday traditions compete with grief. Family bonding competes with discord. And on and on.
Yoga reminds us that there are no winners. We can hold space for all of these feelings—without pushing them away or grasping for them.
Breath, movement, mindfulness, and community are powerful tools that yoga offers to help keep us grounded during the holiday season.
Come flow with us!

                Yoga for Functional Fitness with Catha Pavloff 
December 12, 2026
Functional fitness prepares your body for the movements and demands of everyday life, helping you move with greater ease, strength, and confidence. In this workshop, explore seven key functional movements and learn how to incorporate them creatively into your yoga practice. Options will include gentle, moderate, and more vigorous variations, with and without hand weights and resistance bands. Some yoga experience is suggested. Room temperature is 72 degrees for comfort.
