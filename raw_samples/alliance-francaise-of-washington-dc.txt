Ce Fain!: A Curated Collection Celebrating Contemporary Romanian Design
About the Event: 
This event will be in English and held at the Alliance Française of Washington DC, located at 2142 Wyoming Ave. NW 
This event belongs to the AFDC Series: “Francophonie.” 
Doors open at 6:30pm. 
Join Ce Fain! at the Alliance Française in Washington, DC on Friday, January 22nd for a one-night only, specially curated exhibition and marketplace marking two anniversaries that fixed Romania on the global stage: 150 years since the birth of sculptor Constantin Brâncuși and 50 years since Nadia Comăneci's first Perfect 10 at the Montreal Olympics.
The Exhibition — The Form & Flight Capsule Collection 
A curated exhibition from the Ce Fain!, the capsule draws its line from Brâncuși's sculptural language, the Endless Column, the geometry of reduction, to the flawless form of Nadia’s Perfect 10: sculpture-born fashion, wearable art jewelry, and design objects you won't find on global platforms. Every exhibited piece is available for purchase during the event, cards accepted.
Why this evening belongs at the Alliance Française 
Brâncuși's story is also a French one. In 1904 he left Romania for Paris, where he would spend the rest of his life and become a central figure of the École de Paris, the young Romanian who, legend says, walked away from Auguste Rodin's workshop declaring that nothing grows in the shadow of a great tree, and grew a language of sculpture all his own. From that Paris studio came Bird in Space, the sculpture that flew to New York in 1926 and, when U.S. customs taxed it as a machine part, taught a courtroom what art is.
About Ce Fain 
Ce Fain! is a curated online marketplace created to give international visibility to emerging Romanian luxury designers. The platform connects independent designers with a global audience seeking contemporary design, limited-edition products, and authentic creative voices.
What “Ce Fain!” Means 
“Ce fain” is a Romanian expression meaning “how beautiful” or “how well done.” The name reflects our focus on craftsmanship, design quality, and thoughtful execution rather than trends or volume.
Founder – Raluca Feldenkris 
Raluca, the visionary behind Ce Fain!, is dedicated to elevating Romanian design on the global stage. With a background in digital strategy and cultural branding, she has worked with prestigious institutions such as The Romanian Cultural Institute, The Romanian Embassy in the US, and Alianta, growing their audiences and impact exponentially. Through Ce Fain!, Raluca applies her expertise in storytelling, marketing, and brand development to connect Romanian designers with international buyers who appreciate luxury, craftsmanship, and cultural heritage. Vendors partnering with Ce Fain! gain access to a carefully curated marketplace, strategic visibility, and a sophisticated clientele eager to invest in unique, high-end Romanian creations. By joining Ce Fain!, designers become part of a movement redefining contemporary Romanian artistry on a global scale.
Co-Founder – Andreea Rebaltescu 
(Deeea) Andreea, the co-founder of Ce Fain!, is a dynamic force blending creativity, strategy, and leadership to elevate Romanian design globally. With a background in branding, marketing, and executive coaching, she shapes Ce Fain! into a transformative platform for Romanian creators. As the founder of Sun Media Shine, Andreea has helped businesses and artists craft compelling brand narratives, while her work as a leadership coach empowers professionals to grow with clarity and confidence. Her expertise extends to major cultural initiatives, having spearheaded high-profile campaigns that raised over $3M for humanitarian causes. Beyond strategy, Andreea is an artist and cultural ambassador, showcasing Romanian heritage through music and storytelling at prestigious events. Her ability to merge brand development with artistic vision ensures that Ce Fain! offers its vendors not just a marketplace, but a powerful platform for global recognition and success.

Grand Prix Littéraire Bonjour Books & Alliance Française DC
A propos de l’événement : 
Cet événement sera exclusivement en français et aura lieu à l'Alliance française de Washington DC (2142 Wyoming Ave NW). 
Cet événement fait partie de la série "Des Mots et des Hommes." 
Le prix a pour vocation de mettre en lumière des voix moins connues de la littérature contemporaine française. Nous souhaitons particulièrement valoriser les auteurs issus de la France dite « provinciale », en dehors de Paris, ainsi que les écrivains portant un regard singulier nourri par leur expérience d’immigrés ou d’enfants de l’immigration. Trop souvent, les grands prix littéraires se concentrent sur un cercle restreint d’auteurs déjà reconnus ; nous voulons au contraire élargir le champ et donner toute sa place à la diversité des voix de la France métropolitaine avec une discussion autour des prix littéraires 2026, et l’annonce d’un prix littéraire, le Prix littéraire Alliance Française/Bonjour Books 2026.
Nous renouons avec une tradition d’avant Covid, avec cette soirée pendant laquelle quatre intervenants vont vous présenter quelques-uns des prix votés en 2026 par les jurys germanopratins. Qu’en est-il de la littérature hexagonale ? Hors de Paris point de salut ? C’est le constat de la parisianisation des lettres françaises qui a motivé Jennifer Fulton, propriétaire de Bonjour Books, et Sarah Diligenti, Directeur Exécutif de l’AF de Washington, à créer un Prix qui ferait place aux « Autres Voix».
À travers ce prix, nous souhaitons offrir une vitrine aux auteurs qui incarnent la vitalité, la richesse et la pluralité de la littérature française contemporaine, dans toute son étendue géographique et culturelle.
👉 Cette deuxième édition du prix portera sur des romans publiés en 2026
The prize aims to shine a light on lesser-known voices in contemporary French literature. We particularly wish to highlight authors from so-called “provincial” France, outside Paris, as well as writers whose perspectives are shaped by their experience as immigrants or children of immigration. Too often, major literary prizes focus on a narrow circle of already established authors; we, on the contrary, wish to broaden the field and give full recognition to the diversity of voices across mainland France through a discussion about the 2026 literary awards and, above all, the announcement of the literary prize: the 2026 Alliance Française/Bonjour Books Literary Prize.
We are reviving a pre-COVID tradition with this evening event, during which four speakers will present some of the awards selected in 2026 by the juries in Saint-Germain-en-Laye. What about literature from the rest of France? Is there no hope outside Paris? It was this observation of the “Parisianization” of French literature that motivated Jennifer Fulton, owner of Bonjour Books, and Sarah Diligenti, Executive Director of the Alliance Française in Washington, to create a prize that would make room for “Other Voices.”
Through this prize, we wish to offer a platform to authors who embody the vitality, richness, and plurality of contemporary French literature in all its geographic and cultural breadth.
👉 This second edition of the prize will consider novels published in 2026.

Ortiz’s War: Uncovering a Remarkable True Story - February 12 at 7:00pm
About the Event:
This event will be in English and held at the Alliance Francaise of Washington DC, located at 2142 Wyoming Ave. NW 
This event belongs to the AFDC Series: “Of Words and Men”
Join us at the Alliance Française of Washington, DC, for an evening of literary conversation with author Nicholas Reynolds as we uncover the remarkable, lesser-known story of Peter Ortiz, a Franco-American Marine who became one of America's most fascinating secret agents during World War II.
Reynolds recounts Ortiz’s life, experiences, and missions in incredible detail through his book, 
Ortiz’s War
: From the French Foreign Legion to the Office of Strategic Services (OSS), the precursor to the CIA, Ortiz led daring missions behind enemy lines, worked alongside the French Resistance, and became one of the Gestapo's most wanted men.
Whether you're a history enthusiast, fascinated by espionage, passionate about France, or simply love an extraordinary story, this is an evening you won't want to miss.
Copies of Nicholas Reynolds's book, Ortiz's War, will be available for purchase onsite.
About the Book: 
Born in New York and raised between California and France, Peter J. Ortiz would have stints as a merchant seaman, dude ranch manager, race car driver, lion tamer, circus performer, and Hollywood stuntman and actor. As a teenager, Ortiz ran away from boarding school in France to join the French Foreign Legion. Docking in the US the day after Pearl Harbor, Ortiz enlisted in the Marine Corps. As someone who could speak French like a native–and get by in Arabic, German, and Spanish–the dashing former Legionnaire immediately stood out. Instead of being deployed to the Pacific like the majority of Marines, Ortiz was recruited by the Office of Strategic Services (OSS)—America’s World War II forerunner to the Central Intelligence Agency—for special operations in French North Africa and Europe. In early 1944, Ortiz parachuted into France on an inter-Allied mission. Operating deep behind enemy lines, he traveled throughout Southeastern France—at times donning his Marine uniform—arming and training the Maquis, blowing up Nazi infrastructure, and aiding downed Allied airmen. By the time he escaped across the Pyrenees, he was among the Gestapo’s most wanted. Two months after the Normandy landings, Ortiz jumped back into the French Alps, this time with a team of Americans he recruited, most of whom were Marines, to continue fighting the Axis. When his six-man patrol was surrounded by more than one hundred Germans at the village of Centron, Ortiz faced a choice: fight the enemy or save the village from Nazi annihilation.
About the Author: 
Growing up in the shadow of World War II fueled this author's passion for history. Freshly minted PhD from Oxford University in hand, Nicholas Reynolds joined the United States Marine Corps, serving as an infantry officer and then as a historian. He later served as a CIA officer, ending his career with a years-long stretch as the historian for the CIA Museum. In retirement, he teaches and writes. In 2017, he published the New York Times bestseller, “Writer, Sailor, Soldier, Spy; Ernest Hemingway’s Secret Adventures, 1935-1961."His most recent book was "Need to Know, World War II and the Rise of American Intelligence," designated one of the “Best of 2022” by The New Yorker and a finalist for the Gilder-Lehrman Military History Prize. He and his wife, Becky, live outside Washington, DC and care for a Havanese named Ruby.
