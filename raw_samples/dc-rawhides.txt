Come dance with us! Our goal is to be welcoming, affirming, and fun—to create a dance space where all levels can participate. 
Our dance events usually begin with a lesson, typically a line dance and/or a basic couples dance lesson, followed by several hours of open dancing: a mix of couples dances, line dances, and circle dances (El Paso, Barn Dance).
Dance Etiquette
If you attend, please follow dance etiquette so that everyone can have the best and safest dance experience:
For two-step, waltz, and shadow, follow the line of dance (counterclockwise), with slower dancers keeping to the middle.
For west-coast swing and shadow, WCS is danced in the middle of the floor with the shadow dancers traveling around them, each group being mindful of the other’s space.
At Glen Echo, the stage may be used for west-coast swing when other dances are taking place on the floor.
No drinks on the dance floor. 
Don’t stand on the dance floor.
Dance what the deejay calls.
If you don’t know a line dance, do not get in the way of those who do.
Wear appropriate footwear: no flip flops or open toes.
Do not touch anyone in a way they might consider inappropriate.

About Us
Same-sex country western dancing is a long-standing tradition in the DC area – and the DC Rawhides are committed to ensuring that tradition lives on. Our goal is to promote country western dancing among the LGBT community by teaching and holding dances.
