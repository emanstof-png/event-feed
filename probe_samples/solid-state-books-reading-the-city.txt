Reading the City Book Club @ Solid State Books!
Led by neighbor & urban policy journalist 
Emily Badger
, this book club will range across non-fiction books about housing, transportation, architecture, poverty, urban history, politics and more. 
Join us if you’re curious about how cities work, and how to make them better.
This book clubs meets at our 
H St NE
 bookstore, usually on the 
first Wednesday of the month at 7pm
Remember that book club selections are 10% off in-store in advance of the meeting. Just mention it as you check out !
