Welcome to Blueberry Gardens
Blueberry Gardens provides a relaxed country setting for yoga and Biodanza classes in our octagon. Our three individual treatment rooms provide space for acupuncture, massage, and Reiki. Explore our offerings of wellness related workshops and classes and contact us if you want to schedule your program in our octagon.
Located in Ashton, Maryland, in Montgomery County on the Howard County line, our facility serves clients and wellness professionals in the Ashton, Olney, Brookeville, Silver Spring, Sandy Spring, Rockville, Highland, and Clarksville areas.
