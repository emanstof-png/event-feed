HEALING THE WORLD STARTS WITH HEALING YOURSELF
Eaton Wellness is a holistic wellness center inside Eaton DC at 1201 K Street NW in downtown Washington, DC — open to hotel guests, Eaton House members, and the broader DC community. Services include yoga, meditation, sound bath, infrared sauna, massage, reiki, crystal healing, tarot, and somatic therapy, offered through a rotating roster of practitioners rooted in the city's healing arts community. Private events, corporate wellness retreats, and group buyouts of the full Wellness Center are available by inquiry.

FACILITIES
Yoga Studio
The centerpiece of Eaton Wellness is a light-filled studio inspired by Zen Buddhist meditation temples and the Integratron in Joshua Tree. Acclaimed interior design firm Parts and Labor used custom sustainable organic millwork and living greenery to bring a quality of west coast stillness into the center of downtown DC. The studio accommodates up to 24 people and is available for private events and group wellness programming.
Meditation Room
Our Meditation Room is a tranquil respite in the middle of a bustling international city. Its beauty is in its simplicity where warm light and custom millwork set the stage for uninterrupted focus and higher consciousness.
Treatment Room
Individual sessions with practitioners spanning massage therapy, reiki, 
crystal healing, sound therapy, somatic practice, tarot, and Thai bodywork. 
Each practitioner brings their own lineage and approach — this is not a 
spa menu. It's a community of healers.
Infrared Saunas
Book a private session for yourself or with up to two companions. Sauna sessions run 40 or 60 minutes. No shows and late arrivals are charged at full rate. 48-hour advance cancellation required for a full refund.

Infrared Sauna
Enjoy the benefits of infrared light alone or with a companion in one of our cedar wood saunas. 
Sauna Benefits:
Alleviate of muscle aches, arthritis, and joint relief
Support a healthy immune system
Boost collagen and fight free radicals
Improve oxygen-rich blood circulation
Reduce inflammation
Detox the body
Accelerate cellular regeneration and physical healing
Calm the nervous system
Sauna Information:
We will provide cool, filtered water and plenty of towels for your use, but please bring your own bathing suit or undergarments for your session. Clothing, electronics and personal items may be stored in personal lockers.
Saunas are private, unless you bring a companion to accompany you.
No Shows & Missed Appointments: If you do not attend your scheduled appointment, you will be charged the full price of your missed appointment.
Late Arrivals: If you arrive late, your appointment may be shortened in order to accommodate our schedule. Regardless of the actual appointment time, you are responsible for the full price.
Cancellations: In the event that you need to cancel an appointment, we ask for at least 48 hours notice. If not, you will be charged the full price. If you let us know before the 48 hours, we will give you a full refund.
