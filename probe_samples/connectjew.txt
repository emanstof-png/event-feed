The Flying Lovers of Vitebsk

Before the paintings, poems, and fame, Bella and Marc Chagall were two young dreamers whose love transformed the world around them. Through music, movement, and theatrical magic, their journey unfolds from a small Jewish town into a vivid landscape of imagination, devotion, and resilience. Tender, whimsical, and visually enchanting, THE FLYING LOVERS OF VITEBSK is a love story that reminds us how art and love can lift us beyond the gravity of everyday life. Hailed as “gorgeous” by The New York Times and “a heart-lifting, heart-piercing delight” by The Stage, Ian Ross’ music brings Daniel Jamieson’s passionate words to life for the first time in DC.

Jewish History Walking Tour

We are putting together a small, curated group of Jewish singles for an afternoon exploring the history of Jewish life in Washington, DC—and getting to know one another along the way. We’ll gather for a guided walking tour of the Gallery Place neighborhood led by experts from the Capital Jewish Museum. As we stroll through the neighborhood, we’ll dive into stories of Jewish community, urban change, and the people and places that have shaped more than 150 years of Jewish life in the nation’s capital. After, keep the conversation going over drinks and snacks at a local bar. Israel's 2026 Elections: A Three-Part Educational Series

Join the Jewish Community Relations Council of Greater Washington and The Jewish Federation of Greater Washington for the final program in our 2026 Israeli Election Series, What Comes Next? Israel After the Election. With the October 27 election just days away, what can we expect - and what could the outcome mean for Israel? Join us for an in-person conversation with leading experts as we look ahead to the final stretch and explore the potential paths for Israel following the election. We’ll examine what different electoral outcomes could mean for Israel’s domestic politics, its relationships in the region, and the future of the U.S.-Israel relationship. We’ll also consider the challenges facing the next government and the key questions Israelis and their partners around the world will be watching after Election Day. Whether you’ve been following from the beginning or are looking to make sense of what comes next, this conversation will provide important context as Israelis head to the polls. Refreshments will be served. The views expressed by speakers are their own and do not necessarily reflect the views of the JCRC or Federation.

How to be a Jewish Adult- First Class

This 8-week workshop meets Tuesdays starting October 20, with some exceptions. The full dates are Tuesdays, October 20, 27, November 3, 10, 17, December 1, 8, and 15 from 6:30 – 8:30 pm. Do you ever feel like you could use a handbook for how to build a Jewish life? If you identify as Jewish or Jew-ish but aren’t sure how to incorporate Judaism into your day-to-day life, this 8-week series offers equal parts community-building and deep-dive into core lessons from our tradition to help you feel more Jewishly connected. Led by Rabbi Loren Berman, each class will take on an essential Jewish value or teaching including big topics ranging from love and relationships, social justice, unpacking what it means to be spiritual, and more.

Couples Lab: Jewish Couplehood in its Many Stages

Whether you're newly together, raising children, navigating an empty nest, blending families, or growing older side by side, every stage of partnership brings new opportunities, challenges, and questions. Join us for a four-part series designed for couples at all stages of life and relationship. Together, we'll explore Jewish perspectives on partnership, hear practical insights from a couples therapist and financial planner, and create space for meaningful conversation and connection with others navigating similar experiences. Come strengthen your relationship, learn new tools, and reflect on what it means to build a life together with intention, resilience, and joy. All couples are welcome, regardless of relationship length, family structure, or Jewish background. This series meets on October 20, October 27, November 17, and December 1, 2026.
