Cooking Class FAQs
Is the class located inside the Sur La Table Alexandria store? 
Yes, cooking classes are held within the Sur La Table Alexandria store. To find the exact location within the store, you can look for signage or inquire with one of our associates during your visit. If you need further details or assistance, feel free to give the store a call, and our team will be happy to provide you with more information regarding check-in and class location.
What is the cost? 
Our in-store classes start at $69 per person; prices vary depending upon the type of class.
Can I purchase a gift card for cooking classes? 
Yes! Our 
gift cards
 can be used towards online and in-store cooking classes.Our e-gift cards are perfect last-minute presents.
How do I register? 
Enter the number of seats you wish to reserve in the "Seats" box and click the "Book Now" button. Then enter the name, email, and phone number for each attendee. Finalize payment via your shopping cart.
How long are the classes? 
In-store classes are 2 to 2½ hours in length unless otherwise noted.
What should I wear? 
We will provide you with an apron, but we recommend wearing casual clothes that you don’t mind getting a little bit messy in. We ask that you wear flat comfortable shoes. Please do not wear open-toed shoes or sandals. Please pull long hair back. Unless required by local ordinance, face masks are optional for employees or customers.
Do you offer online cooking classes? 
We do! Online classes are given real-time via Zoom. You’ll need to download your prep packet with an ingredient list beforehand, so you have time to assemble all the necessary tools and ingredients.
