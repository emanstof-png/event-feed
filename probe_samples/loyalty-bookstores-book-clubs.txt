BOOK CLUBS @ LOYALTY!
Image

Come hang out with your Loyalty friends discussing our favorite books across genres, old and new! Led by Loyalty Booksellers and some of our community members, book clubs meet monthly around the DMV. Refreshments included! Books available at 10% off using coupon code BKCB10 online or shop in-store and mention it is for book club! See our full club list below and RSVP on our event pages linked below. You can check out the picks for each club for the next few months below as well. Happy reading and see you at the {book} club!

Octavia Butler is finally getting the recognition as a genius she deserves, but how much Butler have you actually read? And what about the entire generation of afro-futuristic writers she inspired that now dominate the Speculative Fiction shelves at Loyalty? Inspired by the anthology Octavia's Brood, Hannah Oliver Depp and Chantal Tseng will read through Octavia Butler and her literary descendants! Chantal will pair a special drink to match the book each month and Hannah will bring the cultural context of the book's creation for discussion! Join us!
