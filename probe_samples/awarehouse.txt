awarehouse after dark: Cannabliss Meditation 
Saturday, June 13, 2026
7:00 PM
8:30 PM
Google Calendar
ICS
Explore the wisdom and healing power of this sacred flower medicine.
C@nnabis has been a sacred tool for healing, insight, and connection for centuries. In this meditation, we honor it as a plant ally–one that supports deep introspection, emotional release, and expanded states of awareness. This practice is not about escape but about deepening your relationship with yourself, your body, and the present moment. 
C@nnabis Meditation is an energy practice
. Sometimes it’s deeply restful, sometimes activating or emotional, sometimes psychedelic or mystical, but always transformative. 
We begin by preparing the body with gentle movement and grounding breathwork, creating an intentional container for the experience. Before we consume, we will offer a blessing over the medicine and space, inviting you to do the same in a way that resonates with you. Once we consume, we'll breathe a little more and then you'll lie down comfortably to be guided through a body scan meditation. 
This is your time to relax, surrender, and allow the medicine to flow through your system.
To close, we’ll offer space for you to integrate and share any insights of the experience.
This is an energy practice so it is normal–and to be expected–to have any of the following:
crying or emotional release
shaking, twitching or buzzing of the limbs and body
spiritual or supernatural experiences
deep states of rest, relaxation/meditation
You will be guided through the entire experience. Please come with an open mind and a curious heart 🖤 
Experience Price: $65 
Member Perks: 2x & 4x/month save 10% · 8x & Unlimited save 20%
Auto-discount applied at checkout

** How to Prepare for Your Cannabliss Meditation**
1 | Set Your Intention & Complete the Intake Form (this will be emailed upon registration)
Ritual begins with intention. Take a moment to reflect—
What are you calling in? What do you hope to receive? 
Or simply just stay open to meet yourself in a new way. Setting an intention anchors your experience. .
2 | Dress for Comfort & Ease
Wear loose, comfortable clothing that allows you to fully relax. Layers are best, so you can adjust to your body's temperature needs throughout the session. 
3 | Consider a 24-Hour C@nnabis Fast
Consider fasting from c@nnabis for at least 24 hours before the meditation. This act of discipline 
with
 intention can deepen your connection and enhance the plant’s effect.
4 | Create Your Comfort Ritual
Blankets, cushions, and eye pillows will be provided, but feel free to bring anything else that makes you feel relaxed and cozy—whether it's your favorite blanket, a journal, or a special grounding object.

The Four Agreements: A 5-Week Book Club Experience
Tuesday, April 28, 2026
8:00 PM
9:00 PM
Google Calendar
ICS
Join us for a transformative 5-week journey through 
The Four Agreements
 by Don Miguel Ruiz
—an invitation to shift your mindset, deepen self-awareness, and create more freedom in your daily life.
Led by Luisa
, this book club is designed to be both reflective and practical. In our first week, we’ll gather to connect, set intentions, and explore the foundational ideas of the book. From there, 
each week will focus on one of the Four Agreements
, giving you space to fully understand and integrate each principle.
Together, we’ll explore:
What each agreement really means in everyday life
How to recognize where you may be out of alignment
Simple, meaningful practices to help you embody each agreement throughout the week
This isn’t just a discussion group—it’s a space for real-life application, personal insight, and supportive community.
Schedule Overview:
Weekly, Tuesdays 8pm, 4/28-5/26
Pricing:
Free for members
$25 for non-members
Please note:
 You’ll want to have your own copy of 
The Four Agreements
 prior to the start of the book club.
Whether you’ve read the book before or are coming to it for the first time, this experience offers a grounded, supportive way to bring its wisdom into your life—one agreement at a time.

daytime Social: Earth Day Rooftop Edition
Saturday, April 25, 2026
1:00 PM
4:00 PM
Google Calendar
ICS
Join us on the roof deck from 1–4pm for a relaxed Daytime Social in celebration of Earth Day.
This is a space to unwind, connect, and enjoy a slower pace—off the mat and into community.
We’ll have simple potting + planting, and an easy, open atmosphere to hang, talk, or just take it all in.
Planting is a simple way to ground—hands in the soil, body in fresh air, reconnecting to something real and steady. A chance to step out of the noise and into a more natural rhythm.
No structure, no pressure—just good people, fresh air, and space to land in your body while connecting with others.
We’ll close out the afternoon with some sauna + plunge time.
And if you’re not quite ready to leave, you’re invited to stay for a special live music breath session at 4pm—a beautiful way to continue the experience (offered separately).
Experience Price: $35
