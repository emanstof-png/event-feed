After Exit from Hegemony: Ideology and the End of the American Global Order

Six years after the publication of Exit from Hegemony: The Unravelling of the American Global Order, that order is coming to an end. In their book, Alexander Cooley and Daniel Nexon noted the emergence of transnational networks emphasizing illiberalism and nationalism, values at odds with the international liberal order. Since the election of Donald Trump to a second presidential term in 2024, an “America First” ideology has embodied those values, shaping US foreign policy and further undercutting America’s position as a world power. In this talk, Dan Nexon will discuss the critical reception of Exit from Hegemony and how subsequent US foreign policy – and the ideology behind it – are interacting with other global forces to shape the post-liberal international order.
