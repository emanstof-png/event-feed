Chabad Young Professionals
SPECIAL OPPORTUNITIES, PROGRAMS AND INITIATIVES FOR THE JEWISH COMMUNITY IN THEIR 20'S AND 30'S.
Chabad Young Jewish Professionals - Washington DC is the source for all things Jewish in the Young Professionals community here in the Nation's capital. 
​
Featuring vibrant programs from Shabbat dinners and social events to holiday parties and volunteer opportunities YJP has brought together thousands of interesting people from a variety of backgrounds throughout the year. Whatever your sphere of influence, or if your're looking for a new one, you'll find it here. 
​
Recent years have seen a serious upsurge in the number of Jewish young professionals moving to Washington, DC, in pursuit of economic opportunity and career development. The networking opportunities available through our Young Professionals group, bringing together thousands of fellow young Jewish men and women in our vibrant city, and connecting with many thousands of others through the YJP network, offer a family feeling at time when loneliness can easily set in due to work pressures and fluid time schedules.
​
Employment opportunities abound, as many young professionals have found "just the job they were looking for" or "the great job that helped me break into my professional circle" just by shmoozing with other Young Professionals at a Shabbat Dinner or one of the other social opportunities we offer.
Shabbat
Our Flagship Program is the monthly Young Professional Shabbat dinner. A delicious 4 course dinner and socializing with over 100 Young Professionals is the best way to spend Shabbat. 
Click here for more information and to RSVP to our next event.
​
​
Social
Chabad is the place to be for all your Holiday celebrations and parties. Whether with a crowd of over 600 people at our Purim party or spending a Sunday at the slopes hanging out with fellow like-minded YP's, 
check out some of our upcoming events and RSVP by clicking here.

Learn
We offer many learning opportunities and weekly classes. They include: Whiskey and Wisdom - Weekly Torah Study and Dinner, World-Renowned JLI courses, and one-on-one sessions with the Rabbi. Click here for more information and to join a class.
Volunteer
Together with Friendship Circle, Chabad Young Professionals offers various volunteering opportunities. From visiting the elderly and celebrating the holidays with them to preparing food packages for those in need, we offer year-round programs volunteer possibilities.
