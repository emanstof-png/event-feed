Moishe House Washington D.C. – Columbia Heights
Moishe House Washington DC – Columbia Heights is located in the heart of DC geographically, socially, and culturally! As one of the first Moishe Houses, we have established a firm place in DC’s young adult Jewish life. We host seven events a month that are welcome to all Jewish adults between the ages of 22 and 32 in the DMV area. We are excited to have you join an event soon like Shabbat dinner, bagel brunch, or Havdalah. Follow us on Instagram or 
Partiful

 or

 to be the first to hear about our exciting events. We look forward to seeing you soon!
