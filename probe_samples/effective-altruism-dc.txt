Your Next Act, Your Highest Impact: EA DC Meetup for Experienced Professionals
Tuesday, December 1, 2026
5:30 PM
8:00 PM
                  Industrious
(map)
Google Calendar
ICS
Calling all elder millennials, Gen Xers, and Boomers!
​For most people, the path to effective altruism begins in college or graduate school. But as EA grows beyond its traditional pipelines, more people are discovering its ideas later in life, and bringing decades of experience with them into their second, third, fourth, or fifth act.
​Join EA DC for a meetup of experienced professionals, 40 and over, who are using their expertise to tackle the world’s most pressing problems, or are simply thinking about it. Come build community with seasoned peers, talk through career pivots, and explore how to make your next act your most impactful yet.
​Wherever you are with EA, whether curious, aligned, adjacent, or deeply involved, you belong here. The focus is 40+ but all ages are welcome.
​Food and non-alcoholic drinks will be provided.
​Questions? Contact Karen Maria Alston on Luma or email 
kalston266@gmail.com
