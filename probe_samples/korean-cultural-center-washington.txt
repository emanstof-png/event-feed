Kimjang Day 2026: Experience Kimjang & Korean Culture
Event Date
									Nov 15, 2026
						Join the Korean Cultural Center Washington, D.C. (KCCDC), the DC Mayor’s Office on Asian and Pacific Islander Affairs (MOAPIA), the DC Public Library and chef Patrice Cunningham of Taegu Kimchi for Kimjang Day 2026, a hands-on celebration of Korea’s beloved communal tradition of making and sharing of kimchi!   This grand annual event now in its fourth year commemorates the 2022 establishment of Kimchi Day in Washington, D.C., as well as in Virginia, Maryland, and other states, and coincides with the same holiday in Korea on November 22.   This year’s Kimjang event has expanded to a full afternoon of cultural programs at DC's iconic Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial Library and offers a richer and more diverse experience than ever before, including a conversation with local chef Danny Lee, K-Pop performances by N2 Studios, traditional samulnori percussion music, and the signature Kimjang event with chef Patrice Cunningham, where guests can make and take home their own batch of fresh kimchi.   Kimjang Day 2026 Sunday, November 15, 2026, 1 – 5 PM (check-in opens 30 minutes before each event) Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial Library 5F 901 G St. NW, Washington, DC   RSVPs OPEN MONDAY, NOVEMBER 2 at 10:00 AM! About Kimjang   A centuries-old tradition, kimjang (김장) often happens once or twice a year in Korea as a way for communities to stock up on essential preserved foods like kimchi (김치), especially before winter. This labor-intensive process is rooted in the tight-knit rural communities of the past, yet the culinary and social bonding benefits of kimjang remain a treasured aspect of Korean culture today.   This annual kimjang partnership between the KCCDC and MOAPIA marks more than a decade since the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) inscribed Kimjang on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2013. Washington, D.C., Maryland, Virginia, and other states also already recognize Kimchi Day on November 22, as does Korea, and the U.S. Congress is considering a similar national designation.

My Korea Story 2: Share Your Memory — Hear their stories!
Event Date
									Nov 12, 2026

Through their stories, audiences will discover different perspectives on what Korea can mean to people from diverse backgrounds.   Following the storytelling session, audience members will have the opportunity to ask questions, share their thoughts, and engage with the presenters during an open Q&A session.   Whether you have your own connection to Korea or are simply curious about Korean culture and the experiences of others, join us for an evening of storytelling, cultural exchange, and conversation!   My Korea Story 2: Share Your Memory Thursday, November 12, 2026 6:00–8:00 PM Korean Cultural Center Washington, D.C.
