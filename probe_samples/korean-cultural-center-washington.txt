My Korea Story 2: Share Your Memory — Call for Participants!
						Have a special story about Korea? We want to hear from you -- and so do our fans!  The KCCDC is looking for 5 great storytellers to share their unique experiences of Korea in-person at the My Korea Story 2: Share Your Memory event.  Whether it’s a memorable travel adventure, educational experience studing abroad, following your love for K-pop and media, discovering Korean food, or exploring other aspects of Korean culture, we want to hear and share your story. Selected participants will have the opportunity to share their experiences with the local community through a presentation of approximately 10 minutes at the in person event on November 12, 2026 and will receive a $200 honorarium.  Important Dates:  ●Application Period: October 19–25, 2026 ●Selected Participants Announced: October 26, 2026 ●Event Date: November 12, 2026 ●Location: Korean Cultural Center Washington, D.C. Ready to Apply?  Please complete our application form when you're ready to apply!  Questions?  Check back for more details as they are announced! For questions, please email Jiehyun Park at jh.park@mofa.or.kr.

Celebrate Hangeul Day 2026! Craft Your Korean Postcard
						Join us at the KCCDC to craft your own customized Korean postcard in celebration of Hangeul Day 2026!  From October 5 through 16, visitors are invited to choose a design and craft their own unique Korean postcard using stamps featuring hangeul alphabet letters, traditional Korean patterns, and iconic images of Korean culture such as hanbok clothing and dolhareubang statues. Create a custom message or choose a suggested one! The postcard is yours to send or keep.  Visitors are welcome to stop by for the activity, no appointment required, from 1:00 to 5:00 PM, Oct. 5 - 16.  Stop by, get creative, and make your own one-of-a-kind Korean postcard! Celebrate Hangeul Day 2026 October 5–16, 2026 (1:00–5:00 PM) Korean Cultural Center Washington, D.C. (1st Floor)

Celebrate Hangeul Day at Indiana University Bloomington
Event Date
									Oct 08, 2026
						The Korean Cultural Center Washington, D.C. (KCCDC), in partnership with Indiana University Bloomington Institute for Korean Studies (IKS), will present the 2026 Hangeul Day Celebration on Thursday, October 8, at the Indiana University Bloomington campus. Held in celebration of Hangeul Day, the event invites Indiana University students and the local community to discover Korean language and culture through a variety of interactive activities. Participants can enjoy a Korea and Hangeul-themed quiz, tote bag decorating, Korean food, and raffle prizes. Earlier in the day, a Korean Rice Cake Workshop will be held in two sessions at 11:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m. Led by Rice Blossoms, the workshop will introduce participants to the cultural significance of Chuseok and songpyeon, a traditional Korean rice cake, followed by a hands-on opportunity to make their own songpyeon. The celebration will also feature a traditional Korean music performance by the Korean Performing Arts Institute of Chicago (KPAC). Featuring samulnori percussion and gayageum, the performance will introduce audiences to the dynamic sounds and traditions of Korean music. 2026 Hangeul Day Celebration Date: Thursday, October 8, 2026 Location: Indiana University Bloomington, Global and International Studies Building (GISB), 355 N Eagleson Ave., Bloomington, IN Korean Rice Cake Workshop 11:00 a.m.–12:30 p.m. / 2:00–3:30 p.m. GISB 1060 Hangeul Day Celebration 5:30–7:00 p.m. Shreve Auditorium Please refer to Indiana University’s event information for additional details and registration requirements for individual programs.

Korea Week 2026: In Partnership with the University of Michigan and Ann Arbor District Library
Event Period
									Sep 27, 2026
										 - 
									Oct 04, 2026
						The Korean Cultural Center Washington, D.C. (KCCDC), in partnership with the Nam Center for Korean Studies at the University of Michigan and the Ann Arbor District Library (AADL), will present the "Korea Week 2026" from Monday, September 28 through Sunday, October 4. The festival invites the University of Michigan community and Ann Arbor residents to discover and experience Korean culture through a variety of programs. Throughout the week, visitors can enjoy hanbok workshops, traditional Korean games and crafts, Korean ink wash painting, a lecture on Korean food and culture, Hangul activities, traditional Korean percussion, and Korean storytime, with programs designed for both children and adults. A special highlight on September 28 will be “The Beauty of Hanbok: Discovering Korean Culture Through Traditional and Modern Hanbok,” led by professional hanbok designer Laura Park. Participants will learn about the history and cultural significance of hanbok while exploring and experiencing both traditional and modern styles. Through Korea Week, KCCDC aims to expand opportunities for communities beyond the Washington, D.C. area to experience Korean culture firsthand while strengthening long-term cultural exchange and partnerships with local educational and cultural institutions. ※ For the full schedule, locations, and details about each program, please visit the Ann Arbor District Library’s Korea Week event page.
