“She Kills Monsters”- By Qui Nguyen

She Kills Monsters is a thrilling high-fantasy adventure comedy that dives into 90s geek culture. After the death of her sister Tilly, Agnes Evans discovers a Dungeons & Dragons notebook that launches her into an epic quest. Filled with dominatrix warriors, nasty ogres, and 90s fashion, She Kills Monsters is a kick-ass tale of found family, loss, and acceptance, packed with humor, heart, and adventure.

“It will slash and shapeshift its way into your heart.” – The New York Times
