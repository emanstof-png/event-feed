Far-Right France: How the Rise of Marine Le Pen and Jordan Bardella Will Transform Europe and NATO

Right-wing populists and nationalists have been sweeping into power across the world in recent years, from Brexit Britain to the US under Donald Trump, from Hungary with Viktor Orbán to Argentina with Javier Milei.

France is likely to be one of the next countries to fall under the sway of the far-right, which will have incalculable consequences for its European neighbours and for the world. Since the 1980s, author and journalist Victor Mallet has observed the inexorable rise of the anti-immigration Front National – now the Rassemblement National – first under the late Jean-Marie Le Pen and now under his daughter Marine Le Pen, who recently anointed her young and charismatic protégé Jordan Bardella as successor.

Far-Right France: Le Pen, Bardella and the Future of Europe explains how they began to dominate French politics and asks what happens next in a liberal democracy that was one of the founders of the European Union and is a crucial member of the Nato alliance confronting Russia.

Wildfires are an increasingly important public health challenge. The Return of Benjamin Lay

A fearless abolitionist returns from the grave. Experience this staged reading of “The Return of Benjamin Lay”, starring Mark Povinelli. One man. An uncompromising conscience. A fight to change the world.

Join us in Georgetown University’s historic Riggs Library for a staged reading of The Return of Benjamin Lay, starring Mark Povinelli, written by Naomi Wallace and Marcus Rediker, and directed by Ron Daniels.
Shepherd, sailor, revolutionary, and early abolitionist Benjamin Lay returns from the grave nearly 300 years after his death, demanding to be heard by the Quaker community that rejected his radical ideas. A four-foot “David” confronting the “Goliath” of slavery, Lay challenges us to consider what it means to stand against injustice—even when the world refuses to listen.

This one-person theatrical performance brings Lay’s extraordinary life and uncompromising convictions into the present. Following the performance, join actor Mark Povinelli and playwright and historian Marcus Rediker for a conversation moderated by Derek Goldman.

This registration is specifically for the staged reading of The Return of Benjamin Lay. Seating in Riggs Library is limited; please reserve only the places you intend to use and cancel your reservation if your plans change so that others may attend.

Presented by Georgetown University’s Department of Performing Arts / Theater & Performance Studies Program, Disability Cultural Center, Laboratory for Global Performance and Politics, and Center for the Study of Slavery and Its Legacies.
