Discover Inner Peace, Stillness & a Meaningful Life
Kadampa Meditation Center-Washington DC is a modern Kadampa Buddhist temple and meditation center located in Southwest DC. We offer a range of weekly meditation classes, workshops and retreats suitable for both beginners and experienced meditators. Everyone is welcome to attend and no specific experience is necessary.

What We Provide
Kadampa Meditation Center DC offers the timeless wisdom of Buddha in contemporary presentations, which emphasize practical solutions to daily problems, moving us toward deeper levels of insight, inner peace and happiness. Our classes focus on creative and inspired responses to life’s challenges that we can begin to put into practice immediately. Everyone is welcome to experience our wide variety of classes and events.
Meditation Classes 
Discover practical meditation techniques that bring peace and clarity to everyday life. Our classes are designed for everyone. Join us to learn how meditation and Buddhist wisdom can help you develop a happier, more balanced mind.
Retreats & Workshops
​
Deepen your meditation practice and explore Buddhist teachings through our special events. From day courses and weekend retreats to guest teachers and community gatherings, these experiences help you cultivate inner peace, wisdom and compassion.
Outreach
Bring the benefits of meditation to your school, workplace, or community group. We offer engaging sessions designed to fit your needs—either at our center or at your location.
Volunteer Opportunities
Build meaningful connections with the community, develop qualities such as patience and wisdom, and play a vital role in creating a welcoming space where others can enjoy meditation classes and community events.
