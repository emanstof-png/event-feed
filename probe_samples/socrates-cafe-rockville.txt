About us
Greetings, and welcome to Socrates Café Rockville! Socrates Cafés are gatherings around the world where people from different backgrounds get together and exchange thoughtful ideas and experiences while embracing the Socratic Method; the idea that we learn more when we question, and question with others.
Socrates Café Rockville has been active for over 20 years. Recently, we have seen a surge in the number of participants. Because we are always gaining new members, we are reposting Socrates Café’s Rules and Code of Conduct. If this is your first time attending a Socrates Café meet-up, please take a moment to read the following:
Rules:
Employ the Socratic Method when possible.
Two questions discussed per meeting.
One hour per question, with a 15-minute break in between.
Clean-up after yourself if you eat anything.
Help return the tables and chairs to their original locations before leaving.
Groups should be between 4-8 people.
Vacate the venue no later than 9pm.
Code of Conduct:
Allow other members equal time to speak.
Interrogate ideas, not people.
Avoid monopolizing the discussion, and do not speak over others.
Be polite. No cursing, yelling, or personal attacks.
Do not engage in behavior that disrupts people's ability to participate in the group.
If you wish to participant in private conversations, or ones unrelated to the discussion question, please move to seating away from the discussion group.
The rules and code of conduct are designed to ensure that all members of Socrates Café Rockville feel welcome, safe, and heard. If you ever have any concerns about violations of the rules or code, please reach out to myself or co-organizer Suma for assistance.
Thanks, and I look forward to seeing you!
Brian, Organizer.

Socrates Café Rockville Meetup
Sat, Nov 7 · 6:30 PM EST
St. Luke's Bethesda, 6030 Grosvenor Lane, Bethesda, MD, US
Socrates Café meets twice a month. Though this may be considered a "philosophical" group, there are no rules as to what is discussed. Usually, the topics revolve around social concerns, morality, and the first principles of things.
Prior to each meeting we vote online for the questions we will discuss. That way, we will have enough time to ruminate on them and have more in-depth conversations. If you RSVP to a meeting, you may post your question in the event comments section below. I'll send out a survey for voting a few days prior to the meetup. We discuss two questions each night. So you will get to cast two votes in the survey.
When we meet, we break into smaller groups of five to seven to discuss the top two questions. Each group discusses one question for around 45-50 minutes, and we then take a short break. After reconvening, each group moves on to its second question.

Lost Holidays Revival Series: Bonfire Night
Sat, Nov 14 · 4:00 PM EST
7777 Democracy Blvd, 7777 Democracy Blvd, Bethesda, MD 20817, USA, Bethesda, MD, US
Bonfire Night, once celebrated in colonial America, has largely disappeared from American culture, with the last confirmed official celebration taking place in Salem, Massachusetts, in 1817.
Also known as Guy Fawkes Night, the holiday has historically carried a rebellious spirit that sets it apart from traditional celebrations.
This event aims to revive and reinvent that tradition without the commercialism and endless merchandising attached to modern holidays.
It offers a chance to celebrate the season with a clean slate, free from corporate marketing.
At the same time, seasonal “bleed” has pushed holidays and their merchandise earlier and earlier each year. As Christmas increasingly overshadows November, this revival seeks to reclaim November as a true autumn season and create a meaningful new tradition!
If you would like to contribute to the event, please consider bringing a dish to share or consider contributing $5-10 to our GoFundMe to help cover the costs of renting the campfire ring and art supplies. Fall themed food and attire encouraged but not required.
