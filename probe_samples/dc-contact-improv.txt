 is on regular schedule and venue (see below)

Regular Schedule and Venue
T
he Sunday Jam and class 
are regularly held 
at 
Dance Place
 from 2:00 to 5:00 pm
, continuing through 2026.
On regular days 
cost is $14 to $17 for drop-in rate - see Dance Place's adult class schedule. Drop in or you can pre-register at 
Dance Place - Contact Improv
. If you drop-in allow time to register and pay (unless you preregister) at the Dance Place registration desk in the main building.
We only use the payment buttons above on holidays and outside of the regular Dance Place semester, when Dance Place is not open and we are renting. Please do not use it on regular session days.

 note about current our status and exceptional circumstances on various pages, including here and

DC-CI Sunday jam mailing list

DC Contact Improv Facebook group

Every Sunday (with exceptions noted in the status section above) we have a Contact Improvisation Jam or Underscore. See 
the Sunday Jam page
 for details.
On 
the first Sunday of each month
 we share 
an Underscore
 at our regular time, from 
2:00 to 5:00 pm
. (Please be on time - that helps the Underscore work well.)
On 
the other Sundays
 we have a class between 2:00 and 3:00 pm and a regular jam from 3:00 to 5:00.
The classes are
l
ed by experienced CI teachers from our group.
 We're dedicated 
to help newcomers and experienced dancers alike develop and deepen their skills and understanding of Contact Improvisation.
The classes lead into a jam – a free-form gathering to explore contact improv – that continues from 3:00 to 5:00 pm. There often is a closing circle during the last half hour.
Please be 
present by
 2:00 if you're attending the class or the Underscore!
We often have a closing circle around 1⁄2 hour before the end of the jam.

Sunday Jam page
 for details and guidance.
